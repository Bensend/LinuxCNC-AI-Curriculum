## Four distinct propositions that must not collapse
1. **Warning generated** — normal LinuxCNC/HMI/PLC requests an annunciator and its command or lamp reads ON. This is only command/diagnostic evidence.
2. **Warning physically perceivable and understood** — each relevant person, location, operating mode, noise condition and hearing/PPE accommodation must be addressed. This is a human-reception proposition, **not** proven by output bits or a catalog 1 m dBA rating.
3. **Hazard prevented/controlled** — independently qualified guard/ESPE, safety controller, final element, gravity holding and physical stop/energy state must work **even if the warning fails**. A pre-start chime cannot be substituted for personnel-presence detection or a safe stop.
4. **Noise exposure acceptable** — the warning plus process and background noise must be assessed for duration/dose and control under the *applicable* workplace framework. Making a horn louder is not a free safety improvement.

## Cross-machine transfer without false equivalence
- **Press brake:** sheet sweep, Y1/Y2 ram, gravity load, hydraulic pressure and helper stations require independent physical protection; audible pre-cycle signal does not replace point-of-operation safeguarding.
- **Mill/lathe:** spindle/coast, chuck/workpiece ejection and door lock require their own stop/containment proof.
- **Plasma/laser:** extraction and arc/cutting noise can mask warning; optical/fume/fire controls remain separate.
- **Robot/cell/feeder:** blind zones, multiple entry points, trapped persons and restart after fault need physical occupancy/guard and reset design; warning alone is insufficient.
