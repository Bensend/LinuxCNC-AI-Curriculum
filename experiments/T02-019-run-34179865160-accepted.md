## Frozen prediction

For a long queued linear move followed by `G4 P0.75`, interpreter/read-ahead may already be beyond the dwell while the prior motion remains incomplete, but the queued delay must remain behind its `WAITING_FOR_MOTION_AND_IO` precondition. Task may enter `WAITING_FOR_DELAY` only after the subordinate motion/I/O completion condition is satisfied. The observed dwell-state interval must be compatible with 0.75 s under the frozen tolerance.

The independent motion oracle is controller motion status (`inpos`) plus position evolution. `current_line` / `read_line` are retained only as anti-circular evidence and are not accepted as proof of motion completion.

## Behaviorally decisive transition evidence

The accepted trace recorded Python constants for this build as:

- `EXEC_DONE = 2`
- `EXEC_WAITING_FOR_MOTION_AND_IO = 7`
- `EXEC_WAITING_FOR_DELAY = 8`

These values agree with the pinned `EMC_TASK_EXEC` declaration in `src/emc/nml_intf/emc.hh` and the Python binding, which exports those enum values directly.

Representative transition sequence from the committed raw trace:

1. The first captured executor state was `7` (`WAITING_FOR_MOTION_AND_IO`) at `t=0.020233 s`, while `inpos=0`. At that sample `current_line=3`, `motion_line=3`, and `read_line=5`: the interpreter/read-ahead had already advanced beyond the dwell even though the feed move remained incomplete.
2. The executor remained in state `7` through the long move. A sample at `t=10.000425 s` still had `inpos=0`.
3. At `t=10.005517 s`, after `inpos=1` and actual/commanded X had converged, executor state became `8` (`WAITING_FOR_DELAY`).
4. The last delay sample shown in the transition slice was at `t=10.753529 s`; at `t=10.758618 s` executor state returned to DONE (`2`) and interpreter state became IDLE.

This is stronger than merely observing a dwell after a move: it directly falsifies the circular inference that read-ahead reaching or passing the dwell proves the preceding motion has completed. Read-ahead had advanced to line 5 while the independent motion oracle still said incomplete, and Task's explicit precondition state held the delay behind the motion/I/O barrier.
