# 4400-OPB-INTERFACE-04 — A coil driver is not a dry contact, a contactor, or an independent safety output

**2026-10-27 | Source-specific cross-repository safety architecture audit | NON-GRADUATED | DOCUMENTARY ONLY**

## Bounded question, prediction and predecessor check

Can the newly published OpenPressBrake `relay_contactor_driver/REV1_RESOURCE_CONTRACT.yaml` be substituted for the Rev 1 CNC-to-Pilz operational output, a voltage-free handshake, the retained 11K2 field-power switching, or the independent wire-75/wire-63 safety paths? What does a reported `COIL_FAULT_*` establish about a physical contactor or a hydraulic stop?

**Pre-source prediction (INFERENCE):** a smart high-side coil switch exposes a powered load terminal and ordinary electronic diagnostics, not voltage-free COM/NO/NC contacts or contactor position. A generic contact relay may provide a dry contact without a safety-rated output. Expect explicit allocation and supply-domain restrictions; do not assume the reusable coil block is actually populated in Rev 1.

## Failure-path adversarial review (INFERENCE unless otherwise stated)

1. **“The command is LOW, so the contactor must be open.”** False inference: a high-side output can be externally shorted to +24 V (explicit in design basis); coil suppression affects release, and contact welding is downstream of the driver. Distinguish **command**, **coil voltage/current**, **contact feedback** and **physical machine hazard**. No one source proves all four.
2. **“FLT1/FLT2 are healthy, so the safety output dropped.”** False inference: the smart switch detects specified IC overload/overtemperature events, not general external contact position, independent safe stop or gravity load holding. Power-invalid may make ordinary diagnostics misleading ([OPB-ISO-03](4400_OPB_L7_L07_ISOLATOR_POWER_LOSS_FAULT_DIAGNOSTIC_POLARITY_SOURCE_AUDIT_2026-10-27.md)).
3. **“A dry-contact output is a safety relay.”** False inference: ordinary G5Q SPDT electrical isolation is not force-guided monitored safety architecture. Safety feedback/diagnostics must be chosen from the actual safety function and qualified components.
4. **“FPGA reset forces LOW, so restart is safe.”** False inference: reset OFF bias is a useful ordinary default, but does not prove a fresh deliberate start after field-power restoration, or that a contactor has physically released. Existing [OPB-ISO-03](4400_OPB_L7_L07_ISOLATOR_POWER_LOSS_FAULT_DIAGNOSTIC_POLARITY_SOURCE_AUDIT_2026-10-27.md) already covers held-command restoration; do not duplicate a paper bank.
5. **“One resource contract authorizes another machine output.”** False inference: a reusable coil primitive is not allocated by the inspected Rev 1 machine map. New allocation requires a specific load, power domain, physical connector, protection, return, and safety-authority review. No evidence supports silently replacing one of the mapped 8 DO.
