## Promoted 3400 contracts

### ATC / pneumatics

A second real rack ATC now confirms that toolchanging should preserve separate states for pneumatic source readiness, pocket occupancy, actuator command, physical transfer evidence, logical `M61` tool identity and later tool-length validity.

The Funkenjaeger DCNC sequence has six pocket sensors and air-pressure proof, but no direct drawbar-clamped/released witness was found. Local abort paths clean some outputs but no global custom-DOUT physical/logical reconciliation routine was found. Do not teach blind M6 retry.

Repository chronology proves field commissioning changed the sequence after an Auto-mode indefinite pause involving spindle-at-speed behavior and again for neighboring-tool interference.

### Dust / workholding

The DCNC movable dust shoe is a genuine stateful auxiliary mechanism: it preserves prior state, obtains Z clearance, sequences pneumatic outputs, waits on the retract sensor and conditionally restores after M6.

Community evidence shows dust collection may be machine-controlled, timed from spindle state, or continuously owned at shop level. Vacuum clamping commonly has program/manual/VCP command arbitration.

Do not collapse dust collector availability, dust-foot position, vacuum command and vacuum-achieved proof into one signal. A production vacuum-ready/loss/recovery implementation remains source-poor.

### Spindle / VFD

The real WJ200 userspace driver exposes running, at-speed, ready, alarm and communication-watchdog observations separately. The inspected HAL uses at-speed and displays fault, but no evidence was found that READY or the communications watchdog is used as a cut/ATC permissive.

Preserve command, running, at-speed, ready, alarm and transport freshness as separate facts. Ordinary Modbus/VFD status is not safety-rated standstill.

### XYY gantry

Pinned LinuxCNC source closes the negative-HOME_SEQUENCE question:

- same absolute negative sequence groups joints;
- switch/search/latch reference acquisition remains per joint;
- `sync_ready()` synchronizes the final move to HOME;
- a homing failure entering `HOME_ABORT` clears homed/homing state and stops the homing trajectory for **all joints**.

Thus a one-side failed home leaves the machine globally unhomed in the inspected source contract. No synthetic gantry lab is justified for this question.
