# 4400-GUARD-REACH-01 — Reach through/under/over is not stopping distance, guard retention or projectile containment

**2026-10-28 | Original official ISO publisher-scope reconciliation | 4400 safety / NON-GRADUATED / no licensed clause reproduced.**

## Question and exact predecessor check
Can a retrofit team approve a press-brake, mill or cell guard because its interlock stops the machine and the guard fasteners are retained, without checking whether someone can reach the hazard through, under or around the *installed* protective structure? **No.** The propositions are different.

**Predecessors inspected:** [ISO 13855:2024 stop/separation and gravity/emission scope](4400_ISO13855_2024_SEPARATION_DISTANCE_SCOPE_STOP_TIME_AND_GRAVITY_EXCEPTION_2026-10-24.md) already teaches stopping time, protective distance and exclusions; [GUARD-FAST-01](4400_GUARD_FAST_01_EU_ANNEXIII_CAPTIVE_FIXINGS_FALSE_CLOSED_AND_MAINTENANCE_BYPASS_SOURCE_AUDIT_2026-10-28.md) already teaches fixed/movable guard retention and maintenance-defeat pressure; [FORM-HAZ-04](4400_FORM_HAZ_04_NIOSH_FACE_05OK005_PRESS_BRAKE_WORKPIECE_EJECTION_INCIDENT_ADDENDUM_2026-10-28.md) already documents actual workpiece ejection. **Increment:** exact published **ISO 13857:2019** protective-structure reach-scope and its exclusions, contrasted against **ISO 14120:2015** guard construction and **ISO 13855:2024** approach/separation. This is a scope and *physical access-path witness* addition, not a new generic guarding competency or unscored case bank.

## Three separate physical propositions, plus independent safety
| Question | Authority and relevant witness | False green indicator |
|---|---|---|
| **Can a limb reach a hazard around, under, over or through a structure?** | Guard geometry + as-built access-path survey by qualified designer, selected ISO 13857 clauses/actual user population and relevant machine-specific standard. | “Guard switch closed,” “panel looks installed,” “light curtain green.” |
| **Will the guard stay installed and resist the hazard?** | Guard construction/fasteners/attachment, expected loads and real maintenance access; ISO 14120 and applicable Type-C. | “Fasteners are captive” without securement/strength; a flimsy mesh panel called a guard. |
| **Will hazardous motion stop before a person reaches it?** | Exact protective device/approach geometry + complete physical stop-time response and risk assessment; ISO 13855 under its defined scope. | PLC stop request, LinuxCNC feed-hold, ordinary FPGA inhibit, or unmeasured nominal deceleration. |
| **Can gravity, tooling, sheet or broken parts still injure after stop?** | Independent load restraint, ejection/fragment containment or exclusion, tool/part retention and energy isolation, with machine-specific verification. | Calculated separation distance or normal Y1/Y2 stopped status. |

**INFERENCE hazard transfer:** A press-brake rear guard with a low service gap may allow a hand/arm to reach a backgauge; a mesh side guard can have opening geometry that matters even with perfect interlock wiring; a forklift-supported sheet can eject beyond a reach-prevention guard. The first two are *hypothetical inspection prompts*, not OpenPressBrake measurements. The last has direct [NIOSH incident evidence](4400_FORM_HAZ_04_NIOSH_FACE_05OK005_PRESS_BRAKE_WORKPIECE_EJECTION_INCIDENT_ADDENDUM_2026-10-28.md) for a different machine. A reach-compliant structure is not automatically impact-resistant.

## Practical, low-friction implementation
- Start with existing **Stage A E** front/side/rear installed guard drawings/photos and **B** exposure tasks; qualified reviewer marks every plausible access route (through/under/around/over, rear step-in, maintenance-open and material-feed gap) as reviewed, blocked by proven geometry, or UNKNOWN. Do **not** ask an operator to reach into a hazard as a test.
- Ask how real sheets, long tools, helpers, jams and maintenance tasks are handled. If a guard must be routinely removed or the slot enlarged, **redesign access/fixtures/guard** rather than rely on warnings or easy-to-defeat interlocks.
- Separately document reach geometry, independent stopping, gravity/load holding, ejection exposure and fresh restart. Do not replace one witness with another or compute a distance from publisher abstracts.
- For children/public access, do not assume 14+ reach geometry or child upper-limb data covers lower limbs or all sizes. Use qualified risk assessment and suitable additional exclusion.

## Same-session source-depth — ISO 13854:2017 crushing-only minimum gaps
**Predecessor scan:** complete canonical index, research plan and PROGRESS had no explicit ISO 13854 or minimum-crushing-gap lesson. Existing ISO 13855, GUARD-FAST and GUARD-REACH teach stopping, retained guards and reach. This is a fifth exact **scope distinction**, not a new generic guarding competency or exam.

**DOC-CONFIRMED official publisher:** [ISO 13854:2017](https://www.iso.org/standard/66459.html), Edition 2, November 2017, last confirmed **2023**, is published/current. It specifies minimum gaps to avoid crushing of body parts **only where sufficient safety can be achieved by gap sizing**. It expressly **excludes impact, shearing and drawing-in**, which need additional or other measures. [EVS-EN ISO 13854:2019](https://www.evs.ee/en/evs-en-iso-13854-2019) confirms the European adoption based on ISO 13854:2017. No paid numeric gap table was accessed; do not invent millimeters.

**INFERENCE and conditional physical witness:** A working press-brake die closing stroke is not personnel-safe merely by invoking a generic minimum gap. Secondary accessible converging surfaces at a backgauge, crown wedge, support, cover or transfer mechanism may warrant a qualified gap-or-independent-safeguard review. The reviewer identifies each actual moving/fixed surface pair, accessible body part and operation/service mode, then determines whether a gap can adequately reduce *that* risk. If not, independent protective measures are needed. This is separate from ISO 13857 limb reach, ISO 14120 guard structure, ISO 13855 approach stopping, and gravity/ejecta protection. No live finger/probe test, selected geometry, numerical gap, PL or machine release. **UNKNOWN** OpenPressBrake equipment; G0/G1 HOLD; no compute.
