# 4400 IFA-VALVE-01 — Certified hydraulic component is not a certified ram stop
**2026-10-28; original independent testing-body document audit; NON-GRADUATED.** No actual OpenPressBrake valve certification, hydraulic truth table, test or PL result.

## Selection and predecessor reconciliation
The canonical safety index already covers Parker DFplus ordinary enable versus safety, Bosch IH04 analog feedback, HAWE monitored valves, hydraulic holding, EDM and the physical Y1/Y2 release gate. **New exact information gain:** original independent IFA/DGUV test principles distinguish a *tested hydraulic valve's intrinsic safety-related properties* from **fault detection excluded from that component test**, from the **whole safety control category/PL**, and from the **physical gravity-loaded machine**. This is a source-depth extension, not a claim that a certified valve makes a machine safe.

## Distinct scope and the diagnostic omission
**M13** covers safety-relevant characteristics of hydraulic/electrohydraulic directional and blocking valve combinations, including defined switch position after control-energy removal. Its *practical-use note* explicitly says a successfully tested valve still requires meeting the selected control-system **category/PL** requirements and applicable machine Type-C rules. Its **printed p.10 footnote** explicitly states that **fault-detection measures on the tested valve/combination are not covered** by these test principles; additional requirements are generally needed for Category 4, and may be needed in Category 2/3 depending on diagnostics/risk. Do not convert the component test into a claim of achieved diagnostic coverage.

**M12** is narrower: **electrohydraulic proportional/servo/continuous directional valves whose electromechanical transducer supply (or equivalent device) is disconnected by contacts**. The original document describes possible contact-based interruption of the transducer supply or an equivalent independent element such as a pilot-stage switching valve. It separately repeats the practical-use category/PL warning and the **same fault-detection exclusion in its printed p.10 footnote**. Ordinary software enable, analog zero command or a proportional driver fault flag does **not** establish the specific M12 contact-interruption architecture.

Both are **testing principles**, not public proof that a particular installed product was tested, what certification covers, or that the complete press brake is certified. Obtain exact product certificate, covered variant/options, scope, restrictions, service instructions and current validity from the manufacturer/testing body before any such claim.

## Original M13 failure and evidence boundaries
- **Hydraulic return:** printed pp.11–12 expect defined return to the safe valve state on control-energy removal and appropriate behavior on power loss/return. The mechanical return principle, spring reliability, pilot dependency and prevention of unintended reactivation matter; do not assume a spool command proves return.
- **Failure paths:** original printed p.12 enumerates changed switching times, spool sticking at intermediate positions, leaving safe position without electrical command, hydraulic faults, wear, orifice blockage, seal leakage and changing leakage rate. These are *test-design considerations*, not observed faults on the selected machine.
- **Service human factors:** printed p.13 calls for prevention of wrong installation, non-latching/unintentional-actuation-resistant manual override where permitted, protected adjustments, and clear ports/type markings. A service manual-override or swapped connector that is easy to misuse is a real design-review target.
- **Performance envelope:** printed pp.13–14 include return time, leakage, switching pressure/voltage, safe-state retention and mechanical endurance; printed pp.18–21 address heat/cold/humidity, shock/vibration and electromagnetic interference. Manufacturer-specific selected pressure, temperature, life and leakage limits remain necessary. **Do not transplant IFA type-test figures to OpenPressBrake as machine acceptance criteria.**
- **Electrical independence:** printed p.15 discusses residual energy, wiring backfeed and unplugged-system behavior, which can defeat a naive assumption that normal controller `ENABLE=0` means the safety final element is physically safe.

## Adversarial five-layer witness matrix (INFERENCE)
| Layer | Question | Legitimate evidence | False shortcut |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CERT** | Was this exact valve/combination certified under which test guideline, variant, operating envelope and limitations? | Certificate and exact product/options, OEM datasheet/manual, test scope | Brand name or family marketing |
| **ARC** | Is contact-based transducer interruption actually wired if claiming M12 scope? Is normal FPGA driver outside personnel-safety authority? | As-built Pilz safety outputs, independent contactor/valve path and power dependencies | Software zero-current/enable command |
| **DIAG** | What detects a stuck spool, shorted output, failed feedback, shared pilot or leakage? | Separate selected diagnostic design, fault assumptions and verified response | M12/M13 component test alone |
| **PHY** | Do Y1/Y2 actually stop and hold gravity load across intended envelope and pressure zones? | Independent physical motion/holding and energy witnesses | Valve `closed` bit or certificate |
| **CHG** | Has service replacement/manual override/connector/pilot/driver changed the tested assumptions? | Change record, exact SKU, independent revalidation and guarded fresh start | “Same-looking” valve and reset |

**INFERENCE:** A component can legitimately pass its scoped testing yet be integrated with a common pilot dependency, incorrect feedback, a bypassed guard or an unsafe hydraulic hold. Conversely, an M13/M12 guideline does not prove an installed component is defective. Keep product qualification, architecture, diagnostics, physical safe state and maintenance isolation separate.

## Practical low-defeat design
Make the approved safety final-element assembly, keyed connectors, non-latching protected manual overrides, port labels and replacement instructions straightforward. Require an accessible one-page certificate-to-as-built map and an independent physical witness before personnel-exposed operation. A procedure that requires repeated inconvenient guard removal for routine diagnosis invites bypass; redesign access rather than deleting safety authority. Any hazardous testing must be isolated/remote under a qualified plan with personnel outside the danger zone; no energization or intentional valve-sticking tests here.
