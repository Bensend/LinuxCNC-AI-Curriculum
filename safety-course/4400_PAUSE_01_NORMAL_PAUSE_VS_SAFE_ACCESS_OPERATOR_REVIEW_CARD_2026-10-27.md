# 4400-PAUSE-01 — Operator/technician pause versus safe access decision card

**Read:** [pinned M0/M1 source audit](../research/4400_LINUXCNC_M0_M1_PAUSE_RESUME_VS_SAFE_ACCESS_SOURCE_AUDIT_2026-10-27.md) and [M66 wait/timeout card](4400_M66_01_PROGRAM_WAIT_TIMEOUT_AND_PHYSICAL_INTERLOCK_REVIEW_CARD_2026-10-27.md). **Status:** documentary source transfer, no new formal paper-case bank, no scored test or physical proof.

**What to tell an operator:** `M0` means **normal program pause**. It does not promise that the spindle is stopped, the press-brake ram is held, the guard can be opened, or stored energy is gone. `M1` may not pause at all when optional stop is disabled. `M2/M30` normally request spindle/coolant OFF, but still do not prove electrical isolation, standstill, hydraulic pressure removal or load restraint.

**Review the actual machine task rather than a generic button label.** Identify who needs access, what motion or stored energy can injure them, which physical guard/protective device prevents exposure, which independent safety function reaches which final element, what physically proves the hazard stopped/held, and how deliberate rearm differs from normal Resume. For maintenance, establish all-source energy isolation and gravity/mechanical restraint under the selected procedure. A safety PLC program and physical proof are not interchangeable with G-code pause or HMI status.

**Simple, anti-bypass design:** keep production Pause/Resume available for harmless cycle interruptions; provide a clearly identified **Safe Access** workflow that requests a qualified independent stop, waits for the appropriate physical witness before allowing access, and requires deliberate reset/restart with occupancy considerations. Provide convenient access and understandable fault messages so operators have less reason to defeat interlocks. If the machine cannot establish the minimum safe-to-operate threshold, do not operate it with people exposed; any engineering experiments require isolated/remote operation with people outside the danger zone and residual risk explicitly assessed.

**Open OpenPressBrake facts:** actual Pilz/AKAS program, Y1/Y2 independent stop and gravity hold, guard/egress, hydraulic isolation, and deliberate rearm remain UNKNOWN. No M0/M1 G-code change is an acceptable substitute. Reuse [restricted Stage A/OPB-D02](4400_OPB_CUTOVER_02_RESTRICTED_HANDSHAKE_AND_POWER_DELTA_CARD_2026-10-27.md). **G0/G1 HOLD.**
