# 4400-GUARD-IMPACT-01 — Four paper-only guard-retention decisions
**2026-10-30 | PUBLIC UNSCORED | NOT a machine commissioning test**

Read [original Troax/Axelent source audit](../research/4400_GUARD_IMPACT_01_TROAX_2014_2026_FLOOR_ANCHOR_DEFLECTION_AND_TEST_PROVENANCE_AUDIT_2026-10-30.md) after existing 2590/guard reach and fixed-fastener modules. For each, record **hazard → claimed proposition → exact product/test configuration → missing physical evidence → low-bypass repair → stop/restart**.

1. **"No penetration" but a person is 200 mm outside the fence.** A 2014 Troax test reports 345–380 mm total deflection without penetration. Can the worker's position be accepted? Explain why the report alone does not establish direction, dynamic peak or safe clearance; do not use 380 mm as a universal rule.
2. **Replacement mesh on unknown anchors.** Installer retains 60×40 posts but uses different panel height and old anchors near a cracked concrete edge. Identify which original TR-14-003 test conditions have changed, and what qualified anchor/fence evidence is needed before claiming any impact performance.
3. **Headline rating transfer.** A buyer sees current Smart Fix 2,100 J and Strong Fix 8,180 J. Explain the conditional ST20/ST30, double-panel, post/bracket and upgraded-bolt combinations, and why a vehicle-impact barrier table is not a spindle projectile guard rating.
4. **After collision, still upright.** A forklift bends a panel and loosens a post, but a green guard interlock remains closed. Specify the safe isolation, quarantine, structural/guard inspection, replacement and deliberate restart process; make repairs easier than bypassing the fence.

**Adversarial source question:** How can a report dated 2014 cite ISO 14120:2015? State the unresolved provenance issue without asserting fraud or an OEM erratum. **No machine-specific anchors, PL, projectile loads or clearances are invented.**

**Source:** [Troax original TR-14-003](https://old.troax.com/sites/all/files/files/impact_test_report_-_smart_fix_1600j_st20.pdf), [Troax current Smart Fix](https://www.troax.com/in/safety-solutions/product/smart-fix/), [Troax Strong Fix](https://www.troax.com/us/safety-solutions/product/strong-fix/), [Axelent vehicle barrier](https://www.axelent.com/us/safety-solutions/protect/infrastructure/impact-protection/x-protect/impact-barriers/impact).
