# ESTOP-MECH-01 — 12 public technician cases (PAPER ONLY, UNSCORED)

For each case state: **hazard/exposure**, **what the observation proves**, **what it does not prove**, **next qualified no-energy evidence**, **safe restoration/restart condition**. Do not supply a live bypass, disconnection or defeat procedure. All are exercises, not test results.

1. A mushroom visibly latches, but a technician reports the rear modular contact holder is loose. The HMI still reads E-stop healthy.
2. Both NC channels agree during normal operation. A common rear bracket is visibly cracked.
3. The purchase order specifies “2 NC” but the exact assembly has no documentation of mounting monitoring.
4. A new Siemens 3SU1400-1AA10-1HA0 installation-monitoring block is fitted and a first-start permissive is absent.
5. A Rockwell 800FP-MT44PX02S is proposed as a substitute for a Siemens monitored module because both have “self-monitoring” in marketing.
6. A 30 mm Rockwell 800H operator is proposed for a 22 mm 800F panel opening without a new holder review.
7. An operator twists the mushroom to release it and the LinuxCNC run-enable immediately returns TRUE.
8. A panel door was serviced, and a rear E-stop contact holder was disturbed; the machine’s last test was months ago.
9. A safety relay reports healthy inputs, but a loose E-stop head rotates in the panel.
10. A maintainer asks to jumper a nuisance mounting monitor to finish the shift.
11. A qualified inspector has a photograph of a correctly mounted contact block but no evidence of physical Y1/Y2 stopping or holding.
12. An isolated machine has a documented functional E-stop test, but there is no plan for verifying the state after replacement of the head and contact assembly.
