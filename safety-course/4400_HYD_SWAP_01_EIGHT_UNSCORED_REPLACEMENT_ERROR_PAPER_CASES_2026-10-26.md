# HYD-SWAP-01 — Eight unsolved maintenance substitution cases
**Public paper practice, UNSCORED; not a live valve-adjustment exercise.** Cite exact manufacturer/variant and distinguish physical fit from safety function.

1. Two cartridges both fit a T-11A cavity. What does this prove, and what does it not prove?
2. A mechanic applies the old cartridge brand's adjustment convention to a Parker E2*1. What exact manufacturer difference demands change control?
3. A replacement has the same rated maximum pressure but a different pilot ratio. Why does pressure rating not establish equivalent load control?
4. A pilot-operated check is substituted for a modulating counterbalance on a vertical overrunning load. What source warning is relevant?
5. A replacement has a manual release, but the maintenance record omits its restored state. Which personnel-access gate stays open?
6. A technician reports the ram holds position for 30 seconds. Why does this not establish safe personnel entry or indefinite hold?
7. A replacement cartridge has the correct cavity and catalog name but an unverified variant suffix. What should the qualified reviewer do?
8. A repair makes all diagnostics green while the independent Y2 load-holding evidence is missing. May the machine be returned to personnel-exposed operation?

**Rubric for later independent evaluator:** source/variant specificity, mechanism, hazard consequence, evidence boundary, safe restoration. No answer key, score, physical test, PL/SIL or machine release is asserted. G0/G1 HOLD.
