## Exact new OEM evidence (2026-10-29)
1. **ABB Application Engineering LVD-EOTN12U-EN Rev A, 2011-04-07**, original complete two-page PDF https://library.e.abb.com/public/a0762b1e0ab1d2c28525788d00524b4c/LVD-EOTN12U-EN.pdf . Directly inspected both web-rendered pages, lines 0–54. Page 1 explains that a failed-conductive braking IGBT can keep the resistor energized despite ordinary drive stop/enable/interlock/STO; recommends thermal-switch-driven main input contactor for listed ACS150/355/310/550/800/850/ACSM1 categories. Page 2 identifies disconnect, momentary start, resistor thermal switch, contactor coil, power contacts and auxiliary seal-in contact. **DOC-CONFIRMED exact 2011 application note**; diagram is an example, not an approved retrofit schematic, certification or universal rule.
2. **ABB ACS800-PC hardware manual 3AUA0000010601 Rev C, effective 2009-10-16**, original full manufacturer PDF https://library.e.abb.com/public/32b419aa58d44cca9e83097c656f1bec/3AUA0000010601_ACS800-PC_MU.pdf . Directly inspected printed pp.97–98 (web PDF pp.96–97, lines 2525–2554). For **R7/R8 with correctly sized resistor and internal brake chopper**, the manual says an external main contactor is **not required specifically for resistor-overheat protection** because the drive disables input-bridge power flow when the chopper remains conductive. It says **external chopper requires a contactor**; resistor thermal switch is required; fault-input handling can vary by application program. A disabled chopper parameter invalidates the claimed resistor protection. **DOC-CONFIRMED exact family/frame/manual**, not generalizable.
3. **Schneider Electric ATV312 FAQ FA173053, published 2014-01-06, updated 2025-07-17**, direct manufacturer HTML https://www.se.com/eg/en/faqs/FA173053/ . **VW3A7723–VW3A7725 lack built-in thermal switches**, whereas **VW3A7701–VW3A7705 include them**; the latter family's stated thermal-contact rating must not be generalized. **DOC-CONFIRMED exact part suffixes**, not installed wiring.
4. **Rockwell PowerFlex 750 programming manual 750-PM001**, manufacturer-indexed PDF https://literature.rockwellautomation.com/idc/groups/literature/documents/pm/750-pm001_-en-p.pdf . Indexed excerpt distinguishes P384 external resistor continuous watts, P385 pulse watts and calculated thermal protection, with different instructions when customer-supplied protection is used. **DOC-CONFIRMED indexed excerpt only**; complete PDF not opened and no actual parameter value known.

## Reconciliation, not a blanket contactor prescription
The broad ABB 2011 application note and exact ACS800 R7/R8 2009 manual appear to differ on an external input contactor, but the **exact frame/internal-chopper bridge-disabling exception** makes a universal rule unsupported. A selected OEM manual must decide if an upstream physical contactor, internal power-flow interruption, or another approved protective topology is required. A thermal switch that only feeds a LinuxCNC HMI alarm is not evidence of physical power interruption. A thermal-trip contactor is not electrical service isolation, proof of DC-link discharge, a safety-rated stop or a gravity hold. Opening it during deceleration may remove braking performance (**INFERENCE**, exact outcome machine-specific). A Schneider resistor without a built-in switch must not be silently credited with one.
