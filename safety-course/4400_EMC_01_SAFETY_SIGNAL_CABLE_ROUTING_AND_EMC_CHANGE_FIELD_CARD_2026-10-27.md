# 4400-EMC-01 — Safety wiring next to motor and valve cables: technician field review
**2026-10-27 | public, unscored source-review aid; NOT machine authorization**

**Decision question:** After a CNC retrofit, the E-stop/light-curtain input LED is green and the ordinary FPGA reports no fault. A new motor/solenoid cable was added to the same duct as safety test-pulse wires. Is the protective function still qualified? **No evidence yet.** A green LED proves neither installation EMC integrity nor physical stop/hold.

**Original source anchors:** [Pilz PNOZmulti installation 1002265-EN-02 §§3.2, 3.3, 5.1](https://www.pilz.com/download/open/PNOZmulti_Inst_Manual_1002265-EN-02.pdf) explicitly separates voltage groups and warns against test-pulse plus actuator conductors in unprotected multicore. [SICK deTec4 8021645/1SWC/2026-02-04 §§4.4, 6.1](https://cdn.sick.com/media/docs/1/41/841/operating_instructions_detec4_safety_light_curtain_en_im0079841.pdf) requires independent OSSD processing, product-specific common 0 V topology and correct M12 routing. [VACON 100 DPD01666B §5.3](https://assets.danfoss.com/documents/276985/AN361179394701en-000101.pdf) specifies its own shielded motor-cable separation and 90-degree crossovers. **Do not copy any OEM distance as a universal machine limit**; actual installed product manuals and selected routing govern.

**Four things to inspect before any energized work:** (1) device and exact manual/revision; (2) both safety channels, test-pulse source, connector identity and protected route; (3) actual 24 V, 0 V, functional shield and PE, without conflating them; (4) what independent safety outputs physically remove/hold, including ram gravity loads. A cable photograph without traceable endpoints does not prove the circuit.

**Three shortcuts to reject:** 'Both channels have voltage, so both are safe'; 'the Ethernet shield or cabinet hinge is good enough PE'; 'we can fix nuisance trips by disabling test pulses or bridging the safety input.' Each can conceal a common dependency or defeat diagnostics. Separate ducts, readable labels, accessible clamps and replacement cables are cheaper than a bypass culture.

**Commissioning boundary:** A qualified reviewer may assess manufacturer-approved EMC conditions only with physical safeguards, isolation and an independently justified test plan. Never induce faults into an operating press brake, defeat the guard, or expose a person to moving tooling to see whether the input trips. Record safety command, selected controller response, final-element witness and independent physical stop/hold separately. A safety reset does not start motion.

**Source-status:** DOC-CONFIRMED manufacturer statements; INFERENCE retrofit failure paths; SOURCE-CONFIRMED only earlier LinuxCNC ordinary-control boundary; TEST-CONFIRMED none; selected machine and evaluator UNKNOWN/NOT RUN. **OpenPressBrake G0/G1 HOLD.** [Full evidence and conditional EMC-1..6 witness](../research/4400_EMC_01_PILZ_SICK_VACON_SAFETY_WIRING_COUPLING_AND_INSTALLATION_SOURCE_AUDIT_2026-10-27.md).

**Edition/cascade nuance:** The original Pilz installation manual is **2016-05**, not a newly issued 2026 edition. Its §5.13.6 additionally requires separate multicore cables for external PNOZmulti cascade CI and CO conductors; this applies only if that topology is actually used. Confirm the selected hardware and current applicable manual, rather than copying a rule by analogy.
