# HOSE-SHIELD-01 — “It has a sleeve” is not a pressure-containment proof
**2026-10-29 | Technician / designer field card | documentary, not commissioning permission**

A hydraulic hose near a person can have **four different safeguards** that are easy to confuse: abrasion wrap, pressure-containment sleeve, hose-end whip restraint, and independent machine stop/load holding. One cannot be credited for another. The [original Gates/Parker source delta](../research/4400_HOSE_SHIELD_01_GATES_LIFEGUARD_PARKER_UHP_ABRASION_VS_PRESSURE_CONTAINMENT_SOURCE_DELTA_2026-10-29.md) extends the earlier [hose-routing](../research/4400_HOSE_ROUTE_01_GATES_2025_HOSE_ASSEMBLY_ROUTING_CLAMP_TWIST_SERVICE_PROVENANCE_AUDIT_2026-10-26.md) and [pinhole injection](../research/4400_HYDRAULIC_PINHOLE_INJECTION_HOSE_FAILURE_ISOLATION_SOURCE_AUDIT_2026-10-24.md) lessons.

| See | Do **not** conclude | Safer design / document |
|---|---|---|
| Spiral wrap or abrasion sleeve | “It will stop a pinhole jet” | Inspect exact product; route away from people or use qualified pressure containment / rigid barrier |
| Named pressure-containment sleeve | “Any leak is safe indefinitely” | Match exact rated assembly, secure end clamps, know **where fluid exits**, retain easy inspection |
| Hose-end restraint/grip | “It stops all jets” | Verify fitting-separation restraint separately from fluid spray protection |
| Green pump/HMI/valve status | “Hose pressure is zero and ram is held” | Qualified zone-specific isolation/pressure verification **plus independent gravity support** |

**OEM example, not a universal rating:** Gates LifeGuard advertises specified burst/pinhole containment and **up to five minutes** under its product conditions. This is not a five-minute permission for personnel exposure. Parker's UHP guide explicitly says abrasion shields are not high-pressure spray protection and distinguishes containment grips from support grips.

**Safe default:** If the exact installed hose, sleeve, clamp, discharge direction, inspection state or physical hold path is unknown, record **UNKNOWN / HOLD**, keep people out of the hazard and do not operate exposed. Do not feel for leaks, loosen a pressurized fitting, remove a guard to inspect live or stage a burst test. A small injection wound needs immediate emergency medical assessment.

**Human-factor fix:** Make the safe hose route, protective guard and end-clamp assembly simple to install and inspect; locate drain outlets and pressure-witness points away from operator/service positions; use a keyed replacement kit and easy guard reinstallation. A guard that must be routinely defeated to see a leak needs redesign.

**Evidence:** Parker/Gates product statements **DOC-CONFIRMED**, cross-machine application **INFERENCE**, installed OpenPressBrake condition **UNKNOWN**, physical tests **NOT RUN**. Reuse existing restricted Stage A A–G intake, no extra owner form. **G0/G1 HOLD; G2 NOT RUN; G3/G4 NOT AUTHORIZED; G5 HOLD.**


**Electrical false friend:** A Gates sleeve described as “non-conductive” is **not** proof that its enclosed hose, fluid, fittings or end clamps are electrically isolated. Parker 4400-B.1 §2.1.1 requires exact selected/marked nonconductive hose and equipment approval for applications that depend on electrical nonconductivity. Do not replace qualified electrical isolation/verification with a plastic cover or conduct a live test to find out.
