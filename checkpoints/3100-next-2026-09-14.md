## Preserved 3100 conclusions

1. `M6` physical transfer, logical tool identity and `G43` tool-length-offset activation are separate states.
2. M19 has explicit request/ack/fault/timeout semantics; `at-speed`, spindle sync/index and orient/locked are distinct witnesses.
3. Rigid tapping is spindle-synchronized motion, not spindle orientation.
4. VMC ATCs vary materially: conventional independent carousel + coordinated Z versus EMCO spindle-driven carousel with temporary drive-authority transfer.
5. A remapped M6 is a useful transaction/orchestration owner when coordinated machine-axis motion is required; `carousel.comp` can own magazine positioning; physical I/O remains separate evidence.
6. Sensor debounce does not repair a bad sequence, and carousel position does not prove tool-transfer/coupling state.
7. The inspectable ATCduino M6 routine provides real carousel `INPOSITION` and piston-return/enable M66 timeouts, but spindle lock/unlock and piston extension remain dwell-based in the inspected routine.
8. ATCduino's visible `ON_ABORT_COMMAND` does not prove cleanup: direct output-clearing lines are commented and the called `reset_state` definition was not found on the inspected default branch.
