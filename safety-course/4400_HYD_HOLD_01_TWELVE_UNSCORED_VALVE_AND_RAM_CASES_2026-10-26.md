# HYD-HOLD-01 — Twelve unscored technician scenarios
**Public practice, NOT a blind evaluator or safety validation.** No solutions or hidden keys are published. Each response must identify source-supported facts, inference, unknown physical facts and a safe next witness.

1. A machine has a check-valve cartridge but the Parker HAS order code is **N**. What does the exact manual say about holding?
2. A Parker HAS actuator has a **R** pilot check with manual release. What is not proven by the presence of the cartridge?
3. A technician proposes using a PO check alone for smooth lowering of a vertical overrunning ram. Which OEM warning applies?
4. The pump stops and the LinuxCNC valve command goes low, yet the Y axis is loaded by gravity. Which independent evidence is still missing?
5. An upstream Bosch STOM valve is position-monitored and reports closed. Does this prove the downstream cylinder cannot exhaust through a broken hose?
6. A hydraulic supplier advertises **Cat 4 PL e** for a STOM variant. Which exact system and safety function have actually been rated, and what remains unproven?
7. A maintenance worker sees a gauge at zero upstream of a holding valve. What evidence is needed for pressure trapped on the other side?
8. An actuator appears motionless for one minute. Does this establish no slow leakage, no thermal relief and safe access beneath the load?
9. A new manifold is the same envelope but a different model code and pilot ratio. What change-control evidence is needed before service release?
10. A controller has a pressure sensor, valve-position sensor and position encoder. Why might none alone establish physical mechanical support?
11. A qualified rescue procedure proposes depressurizing a load chamber. What must be established **before** anyone approaches the hazard zone?
12. After replacing a load-control cartridge, all diagnostic lights return green. What separates fault reset, safety rearm and deliberate motion start?

**Assessment rubric for an independent evaluator (no answers):** 0–2 for correct product/version scope; 0–2 for physical hazard path; 0–2 for distinct control/safety/isolation boundaries; 0–2 for evidence needed; 0–2 for conservative uncertainty/no-person-exposure handling. Record actual external score separately; no self-graduation.
