## Candidate competencies
1. Distinguish project/version identity, program signature/checksum scope, actual I/O terminal state, monitored final-element state and qualified physical safe-state proof.
2. Detect a misleading apparently healthy diagnostic and identify the shortest discriminating evidence chain.
3. Reason about a standard PLC/LinuxCNC/FPGA retrofit without silently transferring personnel-safety authority to normal control.
4. Treat firmware/module/sensor settings, safety signature, safety-network identity and physical wiring as separately reviewable domains.
5. Refuse exposed-person operation when physical stop/hold/guard/rearm evidence is missing.

## Required learner precommitment
Before the evaluator reveals its independently held oracle, collect primary diagnosis/prediction, mechanism, observable results, shortest verification sequence, alternative hypothesis, falsifier, confidence (0–100%), solve time, allowed resources and safety restrictions. Timestamp and commit this answer; preserve it unchanged. Then obtain independent OEM/source or external execution feedback and score the five 0–2 dimensions specified in [blind protocol](BLIND_FEEDBACK_PROTOCOL.md). Record score, error class, correction, novel transfer and delayed retention separately in FEEDBACK_SCORE_LOG. A reviewer merely agreeing with the lesson is **not** an independent oracle.
