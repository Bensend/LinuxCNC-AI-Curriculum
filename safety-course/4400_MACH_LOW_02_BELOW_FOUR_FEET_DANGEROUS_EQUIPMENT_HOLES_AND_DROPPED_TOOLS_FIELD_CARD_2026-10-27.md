**Human factors:** place service points and tools where they are safely reachable, provide a stable standing position, make covers easy to reinstall with correct retention, and prevent a hole or dropped-tool exposure by physical means rather than a warning alone. Never use the four-foot rule as a substitute for a risk assessment of what lies below.
