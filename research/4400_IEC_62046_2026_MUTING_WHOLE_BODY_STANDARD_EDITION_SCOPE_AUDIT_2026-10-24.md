## Human-factors and hazard transfer
A light curtain that detects a person crossing once cannot necessarily detect someone who has already walked behind it. Muting creates an even clearer whole-body-access question: can a person follow material, enter via a gap, remain hidden, and be present when a conveyor or robot restarts? Treat sightlines, protected-side occupancy, escape, interlocked access and fresh-start authorization as separate physical propositions. These are **INFERENCE engineering questions**, not quotations of the 2026 normative text.
