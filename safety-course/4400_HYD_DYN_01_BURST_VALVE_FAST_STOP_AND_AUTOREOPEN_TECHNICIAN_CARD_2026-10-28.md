# 4400-HYD-DYN-01 — A burst valve can stop a falling load *too abruptly*
**Practical safety-course field card | 2026-10-28 | PAPER REVIEW ONLY, NOT A FIELD TEST OR REPAIR INSTRUCTION**

**The question:** If a hose breaks and a hydraulic fuse slams closed, is the machine safe just because the ram stopped? **Not necessarily.** [Bucher RS 2024 original datasheet](https://www.bucherhydraulics.com/datacat/files/Katalog/Ventile/Rohrbruchsicherungen/RS/RSP-RSW_300-P-9050071-en.pdf) explicitly warns that the fast closure can cause very large decelerations. This does not prove a press-brake failure, but it means the stop event itself deserves a load-path review.

**Four things to keep separate:** (1) detect the hose fault at the actual operating flow, (2) arrest motion without dangerous structural or part-release consequences, (3) hold the load afterward, and (4) prevent an uncommanded restart when pressure is restored. A flow fuse may address part of (1) and (2); it is not automatically a load-holding, personnel-safety reset or maintenance-isolation system.

**Two easy traps.** The Bucher RS family automatically reopens when its specified P/Z pressure relationship reverses; clearing a fault indication does not prove safe rearm. Its datasheet specifies a **40% margin above maximum normal flow for the selected RS closing setting**, which must not be copied to another brand or guessed for OpenPressBrake. The product page says “leak-free”; the 2024 datasheet says **“nearly leak-free.”** Do not translate either into a guaranteed holding time.

**Different valve ≠ equivalent replacement.** Bucher's [CFS technical article (2022)](https://www.bucherhydraulics.com/datacat/files/Katalog/Fachartikel/Sicherheitsventile%20LCW/Technical%20Article%20Safety%20Relief%20Valves%20LCW_100-en.pdf) describes controlled lowering on **excavators**; [DERV](https://www.bucherhydraulics.com/en/products/valves-and-control-block-solutions/flow-preventing-valves/pilot-operated-check-valves/derv) describes a pilot-operated holding architecture with gentle opening. Those are different physical behaviors. Neither is established as the OpenPressBrake installed valve, and excavator numerical limits are not press-brake limits.

**Field reviewer asks for evidence, not a dangerous demonstration:** exact installed valve/suffix and cylinder-port location; all bypass, accumulator and relief paths; normal and fault flow envelopes; qualified independent Y1/Y2 stop/hold and physical pressure witnesses; tool/part retention and structural dynamic load review; power/pressure restoration and deliberate rearm. Use independent mechanical support and qualified isolation for servicing. **Do not loosen a live hose, trip a loaded ram or manually actuate safety valves to “prove” the function.**

**Practical human factors:** Label the exact replacement and setting, provide access for inspection without defeating guarding, make support/isolating the ram straightforward, and make a nuisance-trip investigation easier than bypassing the device. If an operator must defeat protection to finish a normal job, redesign the workflow.
