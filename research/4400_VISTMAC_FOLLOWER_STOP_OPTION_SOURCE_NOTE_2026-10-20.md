# 4400 Vistmac follower normal-stop option — source note

2026-10-20, DOC-CONFIRMED for the cited manufacturer operating guide only. Vistmac's 2024-02-07 *How To Operate And Use The Press Brake Sheet Follower*, §3.4, describes a selectable normal follower response: when enabled, the follower stops when the press slider stops; when disabled, the follower can continue to its target after receiving the clamping-point signal despite a slider stop. See https://www.vistmac.com/how-to-operate-and-use-the-press-brake-sheet-follower/ . The same guide describes connections to press magnetic-scale and foot-pedal signals, but no safety-rated stop or load-holding topology. This is not evidence for an installed OpenPressBrake machine.
