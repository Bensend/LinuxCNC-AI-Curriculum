# 4400 FORM-HAZ-04 — Tool/workpiece fracture, sheet whip and setup-dependent danger zones
**2026-10-26 | Distinct 4400 safety branch | OEM/OSHA documentary evidence | No machine test**

## Why this branch exists
TOOL-RET-01 addressed gravity retention during changeover; TOOL-LOAD-03 addressed local rated force. This branch addresses **dynamic workpiece motion and fracture during an actual bend**. An intact clamp and within-limit software axis trajectory do not independently prove that the sheet, punch, die or helper remains outside a changing danger zone.

## Physical hazard partition
| Hazard | Why ordinary stop/hold is insufficient | Independent evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Long flange whips upward/sideways during bend | Stock can move before ram stop request; person may be outside die gap but within sheet swept volume | Actual stock envelope and support/guard plan for each bend/mode |
| Part trapped between formed sheet and slide | Workpiece creates a *new* pinch against machine frame | Part geometry and guarding/safe handling plan, including helpers |
| Punch/die/workpiece fractures under local load | Rated machine tonnage does not establish tool/holder/contact suitability | Exact material, load distribution, tool/holder/adapter capacity and qualified review |
| Special tool ejector, spring or pivot remains displaced | Stored mechanical energy exists after CNC axis stops | Selected OEM instructions and physical starting-position/energy state witness |
| Previously bent part left in tooling | Next cycle can trap/jam/eject material | Physical clearing/verification, new setup review and deliberate start |
| Changed material rolling direction/cutout/bend radius | Different fracture/tear/load behavior | Qualified process-specific assessment, no generic formula |
| New support arm/robot/helper | Changes workpiece path, whole-body access and restart ownership | Re-map zones and independent safety functions; CHG/revalidation |

## Safety architecture and practical human factors
**Normal control:** CNC bend program, force estimator, sheet-follower trajectory, LEDs and tool database are helpful **planning/diagnostic aids** only. **Independent safety-related functions** protect defined access zones and physical stop/hold demands, using selected validated hardware and final elements. **Physical protections** include correctly rated tooling, supported sheets, stable handling aids, barriers, guarding of moving material and appropriate isolation. These cannot be replaced by a normal FPGA `SAFE` bit or a recipe load.

For large sheets, a stable support/follower designed for the actual workpiece can reduce operator exposure and manual lifting; do not assume any specific follower is safety-rated or can restrain an unexpectedly released part. Put operator/helper positions and sweep zones visibly on setup instructions. If guards or support aids are repeatedly removed because they interfere with common bends, treat that as a design failure: redesign the support/guard/recipe workflow so the safer arrangement is easier to use. Keep changes to spring-loaded/special tooling as explicit evidence-invalidating events.

## 2026-10-28 directly inspected NIOSH FACE fatal ejection incident (source-depth, not new generic mechanism)
[Original OKFACE/NIOSH FACE 05-OK-005-01 five-page report](4400_FORM_HAZ_04_NIOSH_FACE_05OK005_PRESS_BRAKE_WORKPIECE_EJECTION_INCIDENT_ADDENDUM_2026-10-28.md), incident 2005-01-24, directly inspected. A **920-lb** steel lug supported by a forklift was being bent **2–3 in from its edge**; it **slipped off the rear lower V-die edge** and was ejected into the operator/support zone, causing a fatal injury. **DOC-CONFIRMED actual reported event**. This validates a concrete *workpiece-to-die engagement/ejection* hazard beyond generic sheet whip, but is **not** evidence of broken tooling, eccentric overload, unsafe ram control, a measured ejection distance or an OpenPressBrake incident. The machine had warning signs and E-stop but no point-of-operation guarding. Existing FORM-HAZ-04 hazard/guard/fixture and Stage A B/E/F/G witness refined; no new paper case, evaluator score, physical test or compute. G0/G1 HOLD.
