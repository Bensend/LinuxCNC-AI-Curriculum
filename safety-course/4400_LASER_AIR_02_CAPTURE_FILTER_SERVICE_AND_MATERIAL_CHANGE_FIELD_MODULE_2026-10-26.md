# LASER-AIR-02 — source capture is not filter efficiency; optical safety is not clean air
**4400 safety | 2026-10-26 | PUBLIC PAPER PRACTICE | UNSCORED / NON-GRADUATED**

Prerequisite: [NIOSH/TRUMPF original-source audit](../research/4400_LASER_AIR_02_NIOSH_TRUMPF_CAPTURE_VS_FILTER_AND_RADIATION_INDEPENDENCE_SOURCE_AUDIT_2026-10-26.md) and [LASER-ENC-01 optical module](4400_LASER_ENC_01_ENCLOSURE_SERVICE_INTERLOCK_BEAM_SHUTTER_AND_RESTART_FIELD_MODULE_2026-10-26.md).

## Objective
Separate hazardous **beam exposure**, **laser-generated airborne contaminants**, **source capture**, **filtration/adsorption**, **fire**, **maintenance isolation** and **fresh restart**. A product's filter element efficiency and fan-running lamp cannot certify occupational exposure or optical containment.

## Eight unscored technician paper cases
For each state missing evidence, consequence, independent owner, easiest robust correction and what must be independently validated. Do not calculate fictional concentrations.

| Case | Scenario | Distinguish |
|---|---|---|
| LA-01 | CNC HMI shows filter OK because fan motor is ON, but a hose has disconnected upstream. | Fan command/current vs effective source capture. |
| LA-02 | A 99.95%-rated particle filter is installed but operator cuts a new adhesive-backed polymer. | Particle filtration vs gas/VOC compatibility and process-specific emissions. |
| LA-03 | Laser radiation assessment is below limits; workers still smell fumes and report irritation. | NIOSH field evidence that radiation and airborne hazards are independent. |
| LA-04 | Filter pressure is low after someone leaves a service door ajar. | Low pressure can reflect bypass, not necessarily a clean filter. |
| LA-05 | Operators wedge an optical cover to remove scrap, weakening both capture and beam confinement. | Two physical hazard boundaries, ergonomic redesign rather than bypass. |
| LA-06 | An old filter is changed without isolating the fan/laser or identifying captured contaminants. | Qualified energy isolation, handling/disposal, no routine exposure. |
| LA-07 | Manufacturer filter efficiency is copied as the claimed room removal efficiency. | Capture fraction, bypass, gas, recirculation and exposure evidence absent. |
| LA-08 | Beam OFF after a fault, but smoldering debris and a disabled extraction fan remain. | Separate fire/thermal and extraction safe-state plan after optical shutdown. |

All LA-01…LA-08 **UNSCORED**. Not a blind exam.
