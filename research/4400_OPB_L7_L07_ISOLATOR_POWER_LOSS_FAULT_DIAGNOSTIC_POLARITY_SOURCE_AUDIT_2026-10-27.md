# 4400-OPB-ISO-03 — switched L7/L07 field-power loss, isolator truth tables and diagnostic-channel source conflict
**2026-10-27 | Original exact-manufacturer/source audit | NON-GRADUATED | NO PHYSICAL TEST OR MACHINE RELEASE**

## Bounded question and predecessor comparison
What *actually* follows, and what does **not** follow, from loss of the retained 11K2-switched L7/L07 field domain in the proposed ordinary OpenPressBrake digital-output circuitry? In particular, may an FPGA-side low fault pin or a manufacturer isolator's “safe state” be treated as proof that a safety-controlled valve or the press-brake ram is safe?

Before consulting the original ST datasheets, **prediction (INFERENCE)**: field-side supply loss should make ordinary high-side outputs unavailable, but loss of a powered transmitter may force the receiving diagnostics to an inactive-looking value; an unpowered receiving side cannot be assumed to drive logic low. Independently, a switched-return fault may leave nonzero leakage. These are hypotheses about proposed ordinary I/O, not the installed machine.

**Novelty audit:** Existing [SNEAK-CUR-02](../safety-course/4400_SNEAK_CUR_02_LEAKAGE_BACKFEED_AND_FRESH_RESTART_FIELD_MODULE_2026-10-26.md) already teaches leakage, shared return, suppressor and coil-current paths; [OUT-01](4400_ROCKWELL_POINTMAX_SAFETY_OUTPUT_SA_BACKFEED_DETECTED_NOT_SAFE_OEM_AUDIT_2026-10-25.md) already teaches a detected safety-output backfeed that does not remove the load. [OPB-CUTOVER-02](4400_OPB_CUTOVER_02_PILZ_MUTE_GATE_POWER_DOMAIN_CROSS_REPOSITORY_SOURCE_AUDIT_2026-10-27.md) already identifies L7/L07, 11K2 and the separate Pilz-owned final elements. **Incremental contribution is not a new generic backfeed lesson:** exact STISO620/621 supply-loss truth tables and watchdog delay, exact IPS1025H ground/VCC-loss behavior, and an inspectable OpenPressBrake **FLT1/FLT2 channel-label contradiction**. These change the diagnostic validity and as-built acceptance questions.

## Direct original manufacturer documents (DOC-CONFIRMED, exact edition)
1. STMicroelectronics **STISO621 DS13394 Rev 5, January 2025**, pp.4–5, 14–16, Tables 4/5/11, Figs.6/7: https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stiso621.pdf . With its receiver powered and opposite transmitter below UVLO, the receive output becomes logic LOW **after the isolator watchdog timeout**; a receiving side itself below UVLO is **UNKNOWN**, not a guaranteed low. Datasheet watchdog `tWD` is **2–6 microseconds**; power-up `tPOWUP` is 30 microseconds. Fig.6 explicitly marks an indeterminate power-transition region. These are component characteristics, **not** measured machine stopping time.
2. STMicroelectronics **STISO620 DS14110 Rev 3, January 2025**, pp.4,11–13, Table 4/10, Figs.4/5: https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stiso620.pdf . Its receiver is LOW after watchdog when its transmitter supply is absent but receiver remains powered; when receiver supply is below UVLO, output is **TRI-STATE**. `tWD` **2–8 microseconds**, `tPOWUP` 30 microseconds. Its two channels share a transmitter/receiver orientation; do not interchange its table with STISO621.
3. STMicroelectronics **IPS1025H DS13780 Rev 7, August 2025**, pp.3,9–10,15–17: https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/ips1025h-32.pdf . Pin table: **FLT1 overtemperature**, **FLT2 overload**; both are current-source diagnostics, not isolated safety contacts. Section 7.4 says a disconnected VCC may continue to drive an active output until VCC falls below UVLO; Section 7.5 says a GND disconnection turns the channel OFF, but the OUT path can still carry a small `ILGND` current (Table 11 max **0.5 mA** under its stated conditions). Section 8 active demagnetization addresses stored inductive energy after turnoff. Do not infer actual coil holding from that 0.5-mA *device test limit* without the installed load circuit.

### Critical source discrepancy — FLT labels are swapped in one design contract
The older `REV1_ISOLATED_INTERFACE_CONTRACT.md` explicitly maps **IPS1025H FLT1** via STISO621 to FPGA `FAULT_OVERLOAD_L`, and **FLT2** via STISO620 to FPGA `FAULT_OVERTEMP_L`. The manufacturer pin description and later `REV2_PRODUCTION_CONNECTIVITY.md` / `manifest.yaml` instead say **FLT1 = overtemperature**, **FLT2 = overload**. This is a **SOURCE-CONFIRMED internal design-document contradiction**; it does **not** prove the manufactured PCB or FPGA firmware is wired incorrectly. Exact physical netlist, pin direction, asserted polarity, FPGA register/GUI mapping and actual fault stimulus must be independently reconciled. The `_L` suffix also cannot prove an active-low electrical signal: ST's FLT pins are current sources on fault and the cited isolator channels transmit logic levels. Treat diagnostic polarity and power-invalid masking as **UNKNOWN** until final implementation authority and review resolve them. Do not silently rewrite the separate board-design repository from the curriculum lane.

## State-dependent reasoning: proposed ordinary output, not a safety function
| State or fault | What OEM/source establishes | What remains unproved |
|---|---|---|
| Both process/logic supplies healthy | STISO621 forwards command; STISO620 returns FLT2; IPS1025H high-side follows input within its operating conditions. | Final wiring, polarity, load and fault response; physical machine state. |
| L7/L07 process power removed, logic 3V3 remains | STISO621 **process receiver unpowered**: no guaranteed LOW output from that receiver; local field-side input pulldowns are the intended ordinary OFF bias. STISO620 **process transmitter unpowered, logic receiver powered**: receive diagnostic can be forced LOW after watchdog. | Whether FPGA low means **healthy** or **invalid**; actual 11K2 contact state, return paths, load voltage/current and safety-valve state. |
| Logic 3V3 removed, process power remains | STISO621 **field receiver powered, logic transmitter unpowered**: LOW after watchdog, not an instantaneous physical disconnect. | Any pre-watchdog transient, actual IPS IN-node response, final load; separate safety system response. |
| IPS1025H VCC lost or GND wire opens | VCC-loss output may continue until UVLO; GND-loss output OFF but nonzero current path can exist. | Exact installed coil/return circuit, leakage consequence and independently proved hazard reduction. |
| FLT1/FLT2 map not reconciled | Manufacturer and newer source assign opposite meanings from the older interface contract. | Which FPGA/HMI indication corresponds to overload, overtemperature, supply-invalid or stale status. |

**INFERENCE (high-consequence diagnostic masking):** an FPGA-side FLT LOW after switched-field power loss could look like “no fault” unless the software explicitly treats process power invalid as **diagnostics unavailable**, not “healthy”. An actual 11K2 loss of permission should not rely on FPGA diagnostic validity to remove personnel hazard. **INFERENCE (common cause):** a return bridge, field cross-feed or powered-down component path could defeat the intended domain separation; exact route and current must be established from as-built schematics and qualified measurements, not imagined.

## Same-session adversarial source recheck — STISO621 is **bidirectional** (critical clarification)
The original ST **X-NUCLEO-ISO1A1 schematic pack Rev 1, March 2025**, directly inspected page 2 of 4, https://www.st.com/resource/en/schematic_pack/x-nucleo-iso1a1-schematic.pdf , shows the exact two opposite-direction paths of each STISO621: FPGA `QA*_CNTRL_L` → field `QA*_CNTRL_P` **and** field `FLT1_QA*` → FPGA `FLT1_QA*_L`. The STISO620 separately returns field `FLT2_QA*` → FPGA `FLT2_QA*_L`. This is direct **DOC-CONFIRMED** manufacturer topology (the ST reference uses the **-32** IPS variant, whereas the proposed OpenPressBrake primitive retains non-32 IPS1025H; diagnostic pin semantics remain manufacturer-defined).

**Correction to a potentially incomplete mental model:** When switched process power is absent but FPGA logic power remains, the STISO621 **command receiver** is unpowered/UNKNOWN, **and simultaneously its reverse FLT1 diagnostic transmitter is unpowered while the logic-side receiver is powered**. Thus **both** returned diagnostic paths—STISO621/FLT1 after its **2–6 µs** watchdog and STISO620/FLT2 after its **2–8 µs** watchdog—can settle to LOW. Neither LOW is an independent healthy/fault-free indication; both require explicit field-power validity. This is a two-direction analysis, not just a one-channel power-loss argument. The exact manufacturer timing is not a machine response or safety time. ST schematic labels ending `_L` identify logic-side nets; they do not establish active-low polarity.

**Evidence effect:** strengthens OPB-D06/D08's source-to-sink review of **both** FLT1 and FLT2 power-invalid masking and confirms the older Rev1 OpenPressBrake diagnostic-label swap is a **local semantic mapping problem**, not something inherited from ST's reference net names. No physical or FPGA implementation was tested. The independent safety/physical G0/G1 HOLD is unchanged.
