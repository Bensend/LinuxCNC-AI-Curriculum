# 4400-GUARD-REACH-01 — Is the guard actually protecting this access path?
**2026-10-28 | one-pass qualified desk/field review, NOT A TEST OR RELEASE.** Read the [publisher-scope source audit](../research/4400_GUARD_REACH_01_ISO13857_2019_ISO14120_2015_VS_ISO13855_2024_SCOPE_AND_ACCESS_PATH_AUDIT_2026-10-28.md) before selecting standards or dimensions.

| Check (existing Stage A B/E/F/G) | Do not accept | Needed qualified evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Reach through/under/around/over?** | “Guard closed,” a picture from one angle, or nominal mesh size. | Actual installed gaps/openings/edges, front/side/rear approaches, tasks, user population, applicable ISO 13857 clauses and Type-C geometry review. |
| **Guard retained and suited to hazard?** | Captive fasteners alone, a magnetic sensor, or catalog guard photo. | Real fastener engagement, attachment, expected mechanical/impact load and maintenance-use design under applicable ISO 14120/Type-C. |
| **Approach stopped before contact?** | LinuxCNC feed hold, FPGA inhibit, nominal speed, unmeasured time, or one green OSSD. | Independent safety function and qualified actual physical stopping/response, protective-device placement and ISO 13855:2024 scope review. |
| **Gravity fall, sheet whip, fragments/ejecta?** | Any reach-distance or stop-distance calculation as universal containment proof. | Separate load restraint, tool/part support, containment/exclusion and fault/restart/energy evidence. |

**Human factors:** If a sheet, helper, tooling or jam-clearance task makes a guard inconvenient, redesign the fixture, access or appropriate guarded mode so the safest route is the easiest. Do not widen a gap or defeat an interlock to save time. No person should perform a live reach test. For children/public access, do not assume ISO 13857 adult scope covers all child limb pathways.

**Status:** DOC-CONFIRMED publisher scope only; selected-machine geometry, independent stop/hold and impact proof **UNKNOWN**.
