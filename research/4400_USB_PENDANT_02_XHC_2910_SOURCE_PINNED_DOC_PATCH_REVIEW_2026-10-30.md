## What is source-confirmed
1. **SOURCE-CONFIRMED:** `Hal::setReset` sends ordinary `doMachineOn` or `doMachineOff` according to `isMachineOn`; `Hal::setStop` requests `doStopProgram`. HALUI/Task then route these to ordinary machine-state/abort commands. The raw RESET pin is also updated. Neither path establishes physical standstill or safety-rated E-stop.
2. **SOURCE-CONFIRMED:** v2.9.10 man-page lines 304–307 refer to `whb.halui.estop.activate/reset` even though the inspected pendant component does not register those pins. `hal.h` lines 386–387 repeats the outdated advice. The patch removes this misleading connection advice and names the actual ordinary behavior.
3. **SOURCE-CONFIRMED:** `whb.pendant.is-connected` is set after successful USB initialization, not by a separately validated RF freshness watchdog. `is-sleeping` derives from received packet interpretation. The patch says what these pins do **not** prove, without inventing a timeout or safety response.
4. **SOURCE-CONFIRMED static patch check:** the complete draft's six unified hunks matched the exact v2.9.10 source contexts, each old/new hunk count matched its header, and all hunks were applied in-memory to the fetched original texts without mismatch. This checks patch construction only. **TEST-CONFIRMED runtime: none.**
5. **SOURCE-CONFIRMED additional documentation mismatch:** v2.9.10 man-page line 178 says `whb.halui.spindle.is-on` (HAL input) should connect to `halui.spindle.0.on`; the pinned `halui.cc` source instead registers **`halui.spindle.%i.is-on` as HAL_OUT** (around line 630), while `.start` and `.stop` are separate HAL_IN ordinary commands. The shipped pendant example wires the input to ordinary `spindle.0.on`, not `halui.spindle.0.on`. The draft corrects the documented HALUI reference to `halui.spindle.0.is-on` and expressly avoids calling that status a physical standstill witness. This typo also appears in the inspected development man page. [HALUI source](https://github.com/LinuxCNC/linuxcnc/blob/v2.9.10/src/emc/usr_intf/halui.cc#L625-L639); [shipped example](https://github.com/LinuxCNC/linuxcnc/blob/v2.9.10/src/hal/user_comps/xhc-whb04b-6/example-configuration.md#L154-L160).
6. **SOURCE-CONFIRMED two further pin-direction contradictions:** v2.9.10 man-page line 152 declares `whb.halui.feed-override.scale` `(float,in)` but `hal.cc` line 521 registers **HAL_OUT**; the same man page correctly calls it `(float,out)` again at line 228. Likewise the man page declares `whb.halui.max-velocity.value` `(float,out)` at line 230, contradicting its own `(float,in)` at line 149 and `hal.cc` line 522 **HAL_IN**. The corresponding HALUI pins are complementary: `halui.feed-override.scale` HAL_IN and `halui.max-velocity.value` HAL_OUT. The draft fixes both declarations but intentionally does not restructure the duplicated documentation sections. These are ordinary override/velocity-control interface directions, not safety-rated speed limits. [Pendant pin registration](https://github.com/LinuxCNC/linuxcnc/blob/v2.9.10/src/hal/user_comps/xhc-whb04b-6/hal.cc#L519-L524); [HALUI pin registration](https://github.com/LinuxCNC/linuxcnc/blob/v2.9.10/src/emc/usr_intf/halui.cc#L725-L727) and [feed scale](https://github.com/LinuxCNC/linuxcnc/blob/v2.9.10/src/emc/usr_intf/halui.cc#L834-L836).
7. **DOC/SOURCE edition boundary:** the inspected development man page has blob `6cd5f625ab2e0d5ec0f731b013b6d4498cd4496e` and uses `(bool, out)` instead of v2.9.10 `(bit,out)`; development `hal.h` blob `b5c05324b9f20e49bf920c60c45a47fe3f599774` retains the stale comment. **Do not apply the v2.9.10 diff blindly to development**; port the wording with its exact syntax and re-review the branch.

## Safety and human-factors transfer
A cheap pendant is convenient for jog/normal production commands, but a labeled RESET or STOP button is not evidence of protective stop. A builder copying the old man-page text might try to connect a raw pendant button to software E-stop while the built-in ordinary ON/OFF handler still acts. That configuration is a **conditional INFERENCE**, not a verified installed failure. Make the easy correct path explicit: keep ordinary pendant commands ordinary; give operators accessible independent protective stop, validated guard/access functions, deliberate rearm, and separately proven electrical/hydraulic/mechanical final elements. For a press brake, the gravity-loaded ram requires physical holding/stop evidence; for a spindle or mill, coast and stored energy remain separate.
