# 4400-ACC-FIRE-01 — Eight fire/gas/holding adversarial paper cases

**Status: PAPER / UNSCORED / NO PHYSICAL TEST.** [Direct HYDAC original-source audit](../research/4400_HYDAC_THERMAL_FUSE_GAS_VENT_FIRE_VS_PROTECTIVE_LOAD_AUDIT_2026-10-25.md). Do not perform fire, pressure-release, charging, energized hydraulic or machine-access experiments.

For each case, identify **DOC-CONFIRMED / INFERENCE / UNKNOWN**, the energy domain, threatened safety function, human exposure, independent physical witness, and simplest practical safer design. Avoid guessing ram behavior, pressure, PL/SIL or stopping time.

1. **Wrong accessory:** a service note calls the version-4 gas-valve fuse *cap* interchangeable with the GMP6 *plug*. Identify the different original installation interfaces and source limits.
2. **Threshold transfer:** a builder copies the cap's printed fusing range into a generic hydraulic safety shutdown setpoint. Why is this not a justified machine threshold?
3. **Gas versus fluid:** a thermal device vents accumulator nitrogen. A maintenance note concludes that all hydraulic cavities are empty. Identify the unproven pressure paths.
4. **Protective stroke:** a machine is alleged to require accumulator pressure to finish an independent safe stop. How would a fire-triggered gas vent affect the SRS and what is still UNKNOWN?
5. **Gravity counterbalance:** a vertical load is allegedly held by stored hydraulic energy. Does gas venting necessarily make it fall, rise or stay? What independent restraint must be demonstrated?
6. **One-shot replacement:** a fired thermal fuse is replaced after a fire without reassessing guards, pressure zones, other damage or restart logic. Identify stale CHG/VAL/PT evidence.
7. **Operator location:** the fuse's vent ports are next to a frequently used access position. Propose a practical design review of vent exposure, access and evacuation without asserting a generic safe vent distance.
8. **Conflicting objectives:** an engineer proposes defeating the thermal fuse to preserve accumulator energy for emergency lowering. Explain why the vessel-fire function and motion-holding function need an integrated independent solution rather than deleting one.

**Required disposition:** Every selected-machine answer remains **UNKNOWN/NOT RUN** until qualified source/as-built and physical evidence. A public paper response is not blind external competency, not a test, and not permission for personnel-exposed operation. **G0/G1 HOLD**.
