Manufacturer documentary evidence only; not a selected OpenPressBrake safety validation. SEW MOVIDRIVE technology *General information about startup*, edition 05/2026, warns in its product context that SBT does not work on the second brake when both safe outputs are assigned SBC: https://download.sew-eurodrive.com/download/html/33356904/en-EN/4222488410763365721483.html . These claims must not be merged into a universal compatibility rule. Exact installed drive, safety-option, software, firmware, encoder, brake and output topology remain UNKNOWN.

The MOVIKIT English and German editions both overlap at firmware 11 in the statement about retained versus cleared brake-test results after an abort. Exact FW11 behavior is unresolved. An apparently successful diagnostic is not independent evidence of physical gravity-load restraint, guard safety, or safe rearm. No tests performed.
