# EDGE-01 — Eight public contact/run-on cases (PAPER / UNSCORED)

No machine demonstration, no hidden answer key, no external score. For each case identify manufacturer-confirmed facts, physical hazard proposition, what data are missing, how to prevent bypass, and why normal control cannot prove the stop.

1. An edge output changes after a moving gate has already contacted a person. Does that establish acceptable contact force or run-on?
2. A Schmersal STW-SL-N-05 profile is proposed for finger protection. What explicit OEM limitation blocks this?
3. A catalog table gives an STW-SL-N-08 response at 8 mm/s and STW-SL-N-05 at 90 mm/s. Why is direct speed ranking invalid?
4. Two edges are series-wired to one evaluation unit. What fault-detection and PL transfer questions remain?
5. A Mayser STB 1000 × 100 × 150 table lists very different response times at 10 and 200 mm/s. Explain contact travel versus time and why neither proves machine stopping.
6. A technician replaces a narrow edge with a taller bumper but retains the old stop-distance approval. Which dependent PHY/SRS/VAL/PT/CHG items are stale?
7. An edge is mounted next to a hot-chip stream and is frequently blocked by metal offcuts. Which physical/human-factor mitigation is preferable to disconnecting it?
8. A gravity-loaded press-brake backgauge collision is stopped electrically, but the load may continue through inertia or hydraulic/gravity motion. Which physical end-state and isolation witnesses are missing?

**All eight UNSCORED.** No invented machine force, hydraulic truth table, PL/SIL, stop distance or OpenPressBrake installed component. Independent unseen evaluation remains NOT RUN.
