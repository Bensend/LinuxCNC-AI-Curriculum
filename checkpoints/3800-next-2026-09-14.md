## Breadth conclusion

3800 is primarily a **state/sequencing/recovery specialization**, not a complex-trajectory specialization.

The five main machine classes are:

1. automatic saws / cut-to-length machines;
2. stock feeders / positioning stops / bar feeders;
3. discrete indexers / rotary tables / dial mechanisms;
4. transfer mechanisms and multi-station machines;
5. automation cells with PLC/supervisory software around LinuxCNC.

The central teaching principle is:

`mechanism position != workpiece position != workpiece identity != process authorization`

A feeder encoder can know exactly where the carriage is while the stock has slipped. An indexer can report the correct station while the mechanical lock is not engaged. A CNC program can finish while the part is not yet safe to transfer.

### 3800-T1 — transfer / multi-station boundary

Preserve the single coordinated-motion-channel constraint.

Study:

- simple single-axis/discrete station motion through extra joints/HAL;
- sequential coordinated station use;
- simultaneous independent multi-axis stations requiring multiple controllers or a different multi-channel architecture.

Do not pretend a single LinuxCNC instance is a general multi-channel transfer-machine controller.

### 3800-P1 — external supervisor / OpenPnP adjacent study

- machine-position synchronization;

Do not expand 3800 into a full PCB assembly curriculum.

Do not launch these simply because the track has started.

## Promotion boundary

3800 should not produce its final playbook until at least:

1. one automatic saw implementation is traced deeply enough to preserve feed/clamp/cut/count/recovery behavior;
2. one stock feeder or positioning stop is traced with actual workpiece-position verification or a clearly documented uncertainty boundary;
3. one indexer is traced with both position and lock state;
4. one real automation cell establishes PLC/supervisor/LinuxCNC responsibilities;
5. transfer-machine single- versus multi-controller boundaries are defensible;
6. interrupted material-flow recovery is documented without guessing physical state from commands.
