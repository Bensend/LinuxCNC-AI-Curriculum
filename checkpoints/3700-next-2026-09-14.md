## Preserved breadth conclusions

1. 3700 must keep grinding, EDM, and honing/specialty finishing as distinct process families.
2. EDM is uniquely deep because process feedback can directly reduce, stop or reverse coordinated motion.
3. Native LinuxCNC `motion.adaptive-feed` + M52 and external offsets are process-control primitives, not a complete EDM controller.
4. Grinding reuses ordinary CNC motion heavily, but wheel dressing, wheel diameter/wear, spark-out and process recovery are first-class state.
5. Cam/profile grinding remains a later advanced branch; real contact geometry depends on wheel radius and tangent/contact correction.
6. Wire EDM requires explicit wire feed/tension/break, dielectric/flushing, spark-generator, recipe and gap-feedback authority in addition to XYZUV.
7. OpenEDM remains a strong adjacent subsystem source, not LinuxCNC field evidence.
8. Honing remains a useful specialty branch but is secondary to the current EDM deep pass.

## New 3700-E1 source result

Pinned Motion source now establishes the exact negative-adaptive-feed mechanism:

- M52/adaptive-enable authority is separate from the realtime `motion.adaptive-feed` value;
- when AF is enabled, the realtime value is sampled each cycle;
- it is clipped to `+-MAX_FEED_OVERRIDE`;
- magnitude contributes to net feed scaling;
- sign requests the trajectory planner's run direction;
- when sign requests a direction change and `tpSetRunDir()` cannot switch immediately, Motion forces the adaptive contribution to zero so the path decelerates/stops before reversing;
- feed hold and feed inhibit remain separate later zeroing authorities.

Therefore negative adaptive feed is not implemented as a naive negative velocity multiplier. It is an explicit TP direction transition with a stop-before-reverse boundary when needed.

This is directly relevant to EDM gap control but does not define spark, wire, dielectric or recovery state.

The public Sodick A320s chronology remains a high-value field case: X/Y/U/V and wire run/tension were brought up separately, the undocumented OEM spark source was eventually replaced, test cuts were achieved, and gap-voltage adaptive feed became a later development problem.

## Preserve these rules

- `adaptive-feed primitive != EDM process controller`.
- `negative adaptive feed != instantaneous negative velocity`.
- `field/process feedback -> adaptive feed` is a separate layer from spark/wire/dielectric command authority.
- reverse trajectory does not automatically imply reverse-safe process-output state.
- wire EDM != sinker EDM.
- direct linear feedback changes the grinder control topology; it does not erase mechanical compliance/backlash.
- servo grinders, hydraulic grinders and nonround grinders remain distinct architectures until evidence proves reusable patterns.
