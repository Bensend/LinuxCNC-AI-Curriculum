## Four distinct physical propositions (must not collapse into one HMI green bit)

| Proposition | Ordinary LinuxCNC/FPGA | Independent safety and physical witness |
|---|---|---|
| **RADIATION-CONTAINED** for each mode, wavelength and aperture | may show mode and laser-request state; not a safety witness | selected enclosure/guard/window ratings, beam path, opening and reflection analysis, qualified accessibility assessment |
| **EMISSION-INHIBITED** on guard opening, fault, loss of safety power | may request normal OFF | independent safety decision and qualified laser-source emission inhibit/shutter path; verify physical termination and stored optical/electrical energy where applicable |
| **ACCESS-MODE-VALID** when pass-through or service opening changes | may request production mode | exact keyed mode authority, actual affected interlocks, altered accessible radiation class, personnel exclusion, protected access and deliberate reset/start |
| **WINDOW-INTEGRITY-VALID** during service and after a strike | may display diagnostic | exact wavelength/power/exposure rating, damage inspection, qualified replacement and revalidation; for active windows, actual strike-to-emission-termination chain |

## Adversarial failure paths and human factors

- **Class 1/2 label transferred across modes:** machine is benign in fully enclosed mode but optional pass-through opens a beam path. The selected mode, option kit, machine labeling, access boundary and actual radiation classification must be independently assessed. A keyed switch or software 'laser enable' is not proof of protective coverage.
- **Wrong transparent window:** technician replaces a scratched rated panel with ordinary clear plastic to keep visibility. Visible transmission says nothing about attenuation at an invisible laser wavelength; engineering must make the correctly rated, keyed replacement easy to source, identify and install. Treat opaque temporary guard or no operation as preferable to improvising a window.
- **Beam strike on an active window:** product states safety-input isolation in <25 ms, but actual laser extinction, physical barrier endurance and wiring must be validated as a *system*; do not present the catalogue number as a measured stop time. Replace a struck window per OEM instructions; do not reset a diagnostic and continue.
- **Guard defeat for long stock or camera access:** make approved pass-through, viewing and material handling practical; an awkward fixture that forces repeated door defeat is a design defect. Any deliberate mode with accessible hazardous radiation requires a separately justified protective strategy, qualified controls and personnel exclusion.
- **Power loss/restart:** verify safety path fails to emission-inhibited state; restored power, cleared interlock or HMI status must not automatically authorize beam emission. Physical emission and any stored-energy effects are unknown until assessed.
- **Laser OFF versus fire/fume/motion:** emission inhibit does not establish hot material extinguished, fumes extracted, machine axes stopped, or electrical maintenance isolation. Separate protective functions and final elements.
