## Breadth conclusions

1. 3700 should be split into three classes: grinding, EDM, and honing/specialty finishing.
2. EDM is the uniquely deep 3700 branch because process feedback can directly reduce, stop or reverse coordinated motion.
3. Native LinuxCNC `motion.adaptive-feed` + M52 and external offsets are unusually well matched to EDM process control, but they are primitives, not a complete spark-gap controller.
4. Grinding reuses ordinary CNC motion heavily, but wheel dressing, wheel diameter/wear, spark-out and process recovery are first-class state and cannot be treated as incidental macros.
5. Cam/profile grinding is a valuable advanced branch because `eoffset_per_angle` and angle-driven external offsets exist upstream, while real contact geometry still depends on wheel radius and tangent/contact correction.
6. Wire EDM requires more than XYZUV motion: wire feed, wire tension, flushing, wire-break detection, spark-generator state and gap feedback must remain explicit.
7. OpenEDM is a strong adjacent open-source discovery target for spark-generator and wire-tension architecture, but it is not LinuxCNC implementation evidence.
8. Honing is the current best specialty-process branch because a real Delapena retrofit case exposes synchronized reciprocation, spindle relationship, tool expansion and end-of-bore correction.
