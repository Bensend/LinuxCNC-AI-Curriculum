# HYD-BB-BS-01 — Four-function hydraulic safety decision card
**2026-10-26 | PAPER ONLY / NON-GRADUATED.**

| Distinct function | Manufacturer illustration | Independent proof still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Supply isolation | Bosch STOM / ROSS HBB or HBH partial functions | Actual installed monitored valve and power-loss response |
| Downstream bleed | ROSS HBB; manual ROSS L-O-X T1 | Trapped check-isolated zones, load restraint, pressure witnesses |
| Load holding / block and stop | ROSS HBH; Parker counterbalance and PO check under specific conditions | Cylinder-side geometry, pilots, relief, leaks, independent Y1/Y2 hold |
| Maintenance isolation | Personal lockable L-O-X plus full LOTO and positive support | No stored hazardous energy, guards, rescue, deliberate restart |

**Do not collapse these functions into one generic “hydraulic safety valve.”** A system can need holding and energy release at different times; satisfying one may conflict with the other. Qualified risk assessment and as-built engineering determine sequence, independent supports, safety architecture and no-person-exposure validation.
