# 4400-CYCLE-LIFE-01 — A relay's published PL/PFH is not an unlimited contact-life or proof-of-opening claim
**2026-10-28 | original OEM source-depth ADDENDUM to CYCLE-01 and 2550 | NON-GRADUATED | PAPER ONLY**

## Question, predecessors, and genuinely incremental evidence
A maintenance technician replaces a relay output block with a seemingly equivalent one, moves it from a lightly switched interlock to a frequently cycled function, and accepts a familiar PL e/PFH number. Is the safety function unchanged? **No evidence supports that conclusion without exact load, contact history, demand frequency, diagnostic schedule, complete safety-function architecture and physical validation.**

**Predecessor reconciliation (SOURCE-CONFIRMED, repository):** [CYCLE-01 Pilz PNOZ s3 original audit](4400_PILZ_PNOZS3_ON_CYCLE_DIAGNOSTIC_START_FEEDBACK_NOTICE_AND_SICK_RESET_BOUNDARY_AUDIT_2026-10-26.md) already establishes that energized relay contacts are not necessarily continuously checked, plus product-specific monthly/yearly functional checks. [CYCLE-01 learner module](../safety-course/4400_CYCLE_01_ON_STATE_DIAGNOSTIC_GAPS_AND_SAFE_PROOF_WORKFLOW_MODULE_2026-10-26.md) already teaches safe documentary-first proof. [2550 examples](../safety-course/2550_EXAMPLE_ARCHITECTURES_AND_ASSUMPTION_TRAPS_2026-09-23.md) already teach B10d/nop sensitivity and invalid use profiles. [MIRROR-01 addendum](4400_MIRROR_01_ROCKWELL_SUVA_AND_SCHNEIDER_ACCESSORY_SOURCE_DEPTH_ADDENDUM_2026-10-28.md) already teaches contact identity/EDM. **Do not claim any of those general mechanisms are new.**

**Incremental exact-OEM source depth:** Pilz PZE X4P has an explicit output contact service-life *load curve*, a separate maximum mission time, and a periodic opening-diagnostic obligation; the manufacturer also prohibits reusing contacts previously switched at high current for low-current duty. A Rockwell assembled-station catalog page demonstrates that **selector operator, pushbutton operator and contact block** can carry sharply different B10d figures, while that particular discontinued station is explicitly **not suitable for E-stop**. These facts expose two concrete substitution mistakes not captured by a single 'PL e' or 'B10d' label.

**Important reading nuance:** The PZE manual states that when graphs are unavailable its tabulated PFH may be used under the manufacturer's stated B10d/failure-rate treatment, **but it separately says service-life graph limits must be met**. These are not permission to disregard the graph or extrapolate indefinite service. The p. 17 example is illustrative and must not be treated as a general limit for other voltage, utilization category, current or module.

## Causal failure-path audit (INFERENCE, not a test)
1. **Production rate rises:** demand frequency increases; previous B10d/nop and output-contact load-cycle assumptions become stale. Re-evaluate actual contact operations per function, not just machine cycles; account for all functions sharing an output.
2. **Output used for every production stroke:** a contact-expansion relay may now cycle orders of magnitude more often than an emergency-only function. The published PFH cannot be transferred without checking exact duty and service-life curve.
3. **Part replacement with mismatched contact history:** a used contact formerly switching high current is reassigned to a low-current sensing duty contrary to the exact Pilz warning; a contact-number match does not close suitability.
4. **'PL e' copied from expansion block:** base relay, sensors, external final elements, diagnostics and common dependencies are omitted; whole-function claim invalid.
5. **Long ON period:** internal opening check is not continuously exercised; a scheduled, qualified stop/check/restart workflow must be planned without personnel exposure.
6. **False equivalence of B10d entries:** an operator B10d, contact-block B10d and relay-output life graph describe different components/conditions; cannot select the largest figure to characterize a whole station.
7. **Routine HMI 'test' shortcut:** normal LinuxCNC/HAL status and an OFF request are not an independent physical stop/hold witness; an unapproved energized test may create risk.

## Practical architecture and witness design
- Keep a **single versioned contact-duty register** in the restricted machine workspace: exact installed order/revision, safety function, base unit, output/terminal, switched load and utilization category, current/inrush, actual annual operation count, contact history, OEM service-life curve, mission-time assumption, diagnostic demand/check schedule, replacement trigger and responsible reviewer.
- Prefer **ordinary controller/solid-state normal-cycle commands** for frequent production toggling where the selected independent safety architecture permits; do **not** repurpose a safety output as a high-cycle normal motion actuator merely to save an I/O channel. Independent safety retains veto and owns the protective stop.
- Make the approved maintenance check easy to schedule with machine secured, guards intact, positive restraint/energy isolation as appropriate, and clear written acceptance; never tell an operator to defeat a guard or repeatedly demand a live stop as an ad hoc test.
- **Selected-machine evidence needed:** Stage A A–G actual part IDs and wiring, output load/current/operation history, OEM duty curves, independent safety program and final elements, Y1/Y2 stop and gravity holding, protection geometry, rearm/restart, and qualified physical witness. Do not expand the first-contact owner request; conditional Stage B review only after exact selected hardware is known.
