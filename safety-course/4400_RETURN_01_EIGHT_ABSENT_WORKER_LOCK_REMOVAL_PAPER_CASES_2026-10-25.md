# 4400-RETURN-01 — Eight PAPER cases: absent worker's personal lock

1. A personal lock remains at 5 PM; the manager says the technician's car is gone.
2. The lock owner is off-site, but the employer has no documented/trained removal procedure.
3. A written removal procedure exists, but no one attempted to contact the lock owner.
4. The lock owner was contacted after removal but will return at 6 AM; nobody can ensure they learn of removal before work.
5. A supervisor has followed the absent-worker steps, but another trade's lock remains on the group box.
6. Every personal lock has been removed, but an elevated ram remains unsupported.
7. The lock is removed during a test/position step; one worker is still inside the hazardous machine area.
8. A new shift starts with a stale CNC AUTO command and an old 'safe' HMI status. Is this proof of deliberate fresh motion authorization?
