# 4400 NSV suffix / leakage-proposition checkpoint

HAWE D 6335 (08-2025 rev 1.1) page 5 illustrates SAKB-compatible NSV model codes ending in `L`. HAWE D 6368 (06-2026 rev 1.3) page 6 explicitly distinguishes NSV without suffix (with seal, zero leakage) from `L` (without seal, with leakage flow). Both statements checked against rendered manufacturer PDFs: https://productfinder.hawe.com/downloads/D6335-en.pdf and https://productfinder.hawe.com/downloads/D6368-en.pdf . DOC-CONFIRMED product-code semantics; do NOT infer a leak rate, beam descent, holding duration or that the NSV is a machine load-holding safety element. D 6368 describes basic-position-open valves requiring control pressure to close; product behavior is not a complete hydraulic safe-state truth table. Carry exact installed NSV code, sealing/leakage proposition, pilot pressure dependencies, gravity/pressure witness and maintenance isolation as separate UNKNOWNs in `DEP`, `PHY`, `VAL`, `UNK`. Source-limited bench decision: no compute justified. Detailed research pending authorized publication.
