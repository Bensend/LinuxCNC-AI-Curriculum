## Three different physical functions, not one “gas safe” bit

1. **Backflow prevention:** a gas-specific nonreturn/check device is intended to prevent reverse gas movement across its seat. It does **not** itself extinguish an advancing flame (O3). **INFERENCE:** blocked tip, pressure imbalance or an unsuitable valve arrangement can permit an oxidizer/fuel mixture upstream of the torch, depending on topology.
2. **Flashback propagation limitation:** a correctly selected arrestor is intended to prevent a flame from propagating past its protection location (O3/O4). It does **not** prove no gas leak, no downstream residual mixture, no hot surface or safe access.
3. **Gas isolation / pressure and residual energy:** independent approved valves, regulators, pipe protection, cylinder/manifold shutoffs, pressure relief where applicable, and verified residual downstream condition have different purposes (O1/O2). An arrestor is **not** an isolation valve, a pressure regulator, a leak detector or a complete fire-protection system.

**Regulatory scope trap:** §1910.253(e)(3)(ii) separates backflow, flame passage and excess backpressure into distinct requirements for fuel-gas **piping**. §(e)(4)(viii) permits certain *non-machine* multi-torch station-outlet arrangements and explicitly excludes machines. Neither provision authorizes copying a hand-torch station manifold to a CNC machine. The original OEM machine/gas-system design and competent regulatory review govern any selected installation.

## Four authority layers and a conditional automated-cutting failure path

| Layer | May establish | Cannot establish alone |
|---|---|---|
| **Ordinary LinuxCNC/FPGA/PLC process control** | A command to stop torch motion, disable ignition and close ordinary process solenoids; HMI indication and timestamped diagnostics. | That a valve has physically closed, a gas line is empty, flame propagation is prevented, access is safe, or protective-device functions are valid. |
| **Monitoring / diagnostics** | If correctly instrumented, gas-pressure/flow feedback, valve-position indication, loss-of-communication alarms, maintenance status. | Safety credit without architecture, failure coverage, independence and validated sensor-to-physical-state reasoning. |
| **Independent safety-related control** | If selected, designed and validated, demand appropriate gas/ignition/motion shutdown and inhibit unexpected restart. | Passive flame-arrestor performance, total gas isolation, pressure-free hoses or oxygen-safe cleanliness without physical equipment and proof. |
| **Physical final elements and energy state** | Correctly selected approved arrestors, nonreturn devices, isolation valves, regulators, relief, hose/cylinder protections and physical exclusion/ventilation. | Personnel access automatically: residual gas, hot metal, ignition, moving gantry and adjacent combustibles remain separate hazards. |

**Adversarial sequence (INFERENCE, NOT AN OBSERVED FAULT):** ordinary CNC program requests torch OFF; a solenoid command drops but the actual valve is stuck/leaking, or a hose retains a combustible mixture; HMI shows OFF; a person assumes the line is isolated and opens an enclosure or starts maintenance; another ignition source or a torch-side backfire occurs. A check valve alone cannot be credited for arresting flame, and an arrestor alone cannot be credited for isolating gas. **Do not propose a live flashback, hose reversal, gas leak, flame, or pressure test as a curriculum lab.** Use existing OEM records and qualified non-hazardous documentary review first.

**Change/restart counterexample (INFERENCE):** after power loss, ordinary FPGA output state, valve default state, gas pressure, gas still in hoses and safety-system reset are distinct. The system should require independently qualified safe conditions and a deliberate fresh start; an old cycle-start bit or recovered power must not count as new operator intent. No machine-specific valve truth table is known.

## Human factors and minimum operation threshold

- Make correct gas-specific fittings, labeled two-gas shutoffs, arrestor inspection access and protected hose routing easier than improvised adapters, hidden shutoffs or bypassing a nuisance device.
- Give a clear **“process commanded OFF / gas physically isolated / residual line status UNKNOWN / hot-work access permitted”** distinction; do not display a single reassuring green “safe” state when independent evidence is missing.
- If an arrestor trips or a flashback is suspected, isolate/withdraw equipment per the selected OEM/emergency plan and obtain competent inspection; **never** treat resetting a LinuxCNC alarm as sufficient.
- If a machine lacks a credible minimum protection for fuel/oxygen backflow, flashback, leak, ignition and access hazards, **do not operate with people exposed**. Any justified experimental activity requires a separately engineered isolated/remote arrangement and explicit residual-risk review, not an improvised flame experiment.
