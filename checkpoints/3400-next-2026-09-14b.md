## Interrupted M6 finding

The inspected `groot.ini` has its `ON_ABORT_COMMAND=O <on_abort> call` line commented out. The preserved `on_abort.ngc` only restores G90/G40/G49; it does not reconcile drawbar output, tool presence, pocket state, air pressure or tool length.

Do not teach a blind `retry M6` recovery. A failed/interrupted cycle can leave several different physical/logical states, including a physically clamped tool whose logical M61 update or length measurement never completed.

## Generic LinuxCNC abort/DOUT source result

At the pinned LinuxCNC revision:

- `emcTaskAbort()` calls Motion abort and clears interpreter/task execution state;
- realtime `EMCMOT_ABORT` stops the active trajectory/jog/homing paths and clears motion execution/error state, but its inspected case does not explicitly reset current `motion.digital-out-NN` values;
- `Task::emcIoAbort()` explicitly clears **built-in** `iocontrol.0.tool-change` and `iocontrol.0.tool-prepare` outputs;
- LinuxCNC has an upstream regression test for abort-during-built-in-toolchange so that I/O status returns to DONE;
- this built-in iocontrol cleanup must not be generalized to arbitrary M64/M65-driven Motion DOUTs used by custom remaps.

Bounded conclusion: a custom router ATC must own its physical output/recovery contract. Source does not justify assuming a generic program abort will reconcile its arbitrary drawbar DOUT.

Do not overstate this into a claim about every E-stop/machine-off/startup transition; those remain separate transitions.

## Safety boundary
