# 4400-LOTO-TEAM-01 — 12 unscored technician cases

**PAPER ONLY / UNSCORED.** These are learner-visible practice prompts, **not** blind-evaluation results, physical tests, or machine permission. Refer to [source audit](../research/4400_OSHA_GROUP_LOTO_SHIFT_CHANGE_REACCUMULATION_AND_TEST_POSITION_AUDIT_2026-10-25.md) and [module](4400_LOTO_TEAM_01_GROUP_LOCKBOX_SHIFT_HANDOFF_AND_TEST_POSITION_MODULE_2026-10-25.md). For each case identify **hazard → affected people → physical energy path → personal control → source-backed obligation → UNKNOWN selected-machine facts → safest next action**.

1. Crew A has two personal locks on the group box. One leaves while another is under the ram. The supervisor asks to remove the job lock for a quick test.
2. Crew B arrives. Crew A's isolation verification is dated last shift. The pump is off, and the ram appears stationary.
3. An electrician wants to verify every disconnect personally, but the primary says only supervisors may inspect the isolators.
4. The primary has verified all isolators this shift. Another employee claims OSHA requires each worker to perform an identical physical walkdown. Distinguish requirement from individual right.
5. A hydraulic technician sees zero pressure on a single pump manifold gauge. A hose downstream of a check valve must be opened.
6. Pressure was zero at morning verification; an isolated cylinder might be repressurized by a cross-connected branch. Is the original check enough?
7. A contractor brings a lockbox and tells the shop's crew not to worry about the contractor's procedure.
8. Two trades have separate boxes and nobody knows whether the other crew remains exposed.
9. A jog is needed to align a fixture. One person is still in the pinch zone; someone proposes leaving locks on the box but enabling the drive.
10. A temporary powered test finishes; the operator returns LinuxCNC to IDLE and tells technicians they can resume work without reapplying isolation.
11. A mechanical ram support is installed, but the team wants to use an undocumented manual lowering screw for rescue.
12. The machine has one electric disconnect, a hydraulic accumulator and a suspended beam. Someone invokes the single-source/no-stored-energy exception to skip a written isolation procedure.

**Adversarial scoring rubric for an independent evaluator:** 0–2 each for (a) recognizing physical versus control authority, (b) correct personal/group lock handling, (c) shift and verification nuance, (d) stored energy/reaccumulation/test positioning, (e) explicit uncertainty and refusal of personnel-exposed operation without evidence. Total /10. **No scores have been awarded.** A fresh evaluator must supply an unseen novel case and retain the scoring oracle separately; do not self-grade this public set as blind.
