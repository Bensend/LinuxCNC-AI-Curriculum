# 4400-G0-PROVENANCE-01 — Public evidence absence is not proof of no restricted submission

**2026-10-29 | SOURCE-CONFIRMED repository wording audit and correction | NON-GRADUATED.**

## Precise issue
The first-contact [Stage A A–G request](../safety-course/4400_OPENPRESSBRAKE_G0_EVIDENCE_REQUEST_ONE_PAGE_2026-10-25.md) originally declared `NO SELECTED-MACHINE EVIDENCE RECEIVED` and `No G0 evidence received`; the [one-screen status board](../safety-course/4400_G0_BOARD_01_ONE_SCREEN_RESTRICTED_EVIDENCE_STATUS_TEMPLATE_2026-10-26.md) said `all UNKNOWN (no owner submission)` and repeated `None received` in each public pointer. These are **stronger claims than the public curriculum can independently verify** when machine drawings, safety-PLC project and evidence are deliberately kept in a restricted workspace.

The course can truthfully say **no qualified redacted status/proof is established in this public curriculum**, but cannot infer that no owner has supplied anything privately. A qualified reviewer might have received material but not yet published a redacted status. Conversely, an undocumented private claim cannot lift a public or physical gate.

## Narrow source correction
- Stage A remains the same seven packets, same owner roles and same first-contact length/architecture; replace categorical public receipt statements with **not established in this public curriculum / restricted status unknown**.
- Board retains all seven rows and gate values, but public pointer means **no verified public status pointer**, not an assertion about private workspace contents.
- No new questionnaire, no request for private drawings on GitHub, no owner contact or physical machine work. No change to the actual qualified reviewer acceptance or G0/G1 HOLD.

## Evidence classes and safety decision
**SOURCE-CONFIRMED:** wording and the public/restricted boundary in those exact files. **INFERENCE:** narrowing receipt language avoids false claims about a private workspace and supports honest owner communication. **UNKNOWN:** whether restricted A–G records exist, their completeness, revision, qualified review, and actual physical stop/hold. **TEST-CONFIRMED:** none. This is an information-governance repair, not evidence that the machine is safe.

**G0/G1 HOLD; G2 NOT RUN; G3/G4 NOT AUTHORIZED; G5 HOLD.** Only a qualified reviewer can reconcile restricted evidence and publish a redacted status, without transferring personnel-safety authority to ordinary LinuxCNC/FPGA or disclosing proprietary details.
