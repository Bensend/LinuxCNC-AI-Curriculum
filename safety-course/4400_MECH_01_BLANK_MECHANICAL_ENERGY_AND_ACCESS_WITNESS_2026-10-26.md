## Distinct propositions
- **BODY-RETENTION:** upper balancer body remains captured after upper hanger failure.
- **TOOL-RETENTION:** load remains captured after lower cable/hook failure.
- **SPRING-ARREST:** internal drum arrests after spring failure under OEM scope.
- **COVER-HOLD:** raised guard cannot fall onto person under foreseeable gas-spring/support failure.
- **ENERGY-SERVICE:** stored gas/spring energy and gravity are controlled for maintenance.
- **REARM:** lock/support removal and reset do not accidentally initiate hazardous motion.

One witness does not prove all six. A safety status bit does not prove any physical load path. **Do not copy** ENDO's 10x auxiliary-chain strength and 100mm slack requirement to unrelated equipment; both are product-specific instructions. Do not invent ratings, travel, forces, hydraulic states or stop times.
