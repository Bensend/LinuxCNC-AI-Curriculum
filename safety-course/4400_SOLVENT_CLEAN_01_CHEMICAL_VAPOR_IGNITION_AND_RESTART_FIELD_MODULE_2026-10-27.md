## Learning objective
For a solvent/degreasing cleaning job, separate (1) motion/guard safety, (2) electrical/hot-surface ignition, (3) vapor and breathing-zone exposure, (4) liquid/contaminated-waste handling, and (5) deliberate rearm. Select a usable low-hazard cleaning method without inventing a universal flashpoint, LEL threshold, fan sequence or ventilation rate.

## Safety and grading boundary
No physical ignition or exposure experiment is authorized. If an exposed machine cannot meet a basic safe-to-operate threshold, keep people outside the hazard; any justified experimental operation must be isolated/remote with residual risk explicit. **UNSCORED** means these are learner exercises, not independent competency evidence.
