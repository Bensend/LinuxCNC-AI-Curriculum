# 4400-EMC-01 — Safety signal wiring, actuator coupling and installation-specific EMC proof
**2026-10-27 | source-only safety engineering | NON-GRADUATED | G0/G1 HOLD**

## Question and predecessor comparison
A LinuxCNC/OpenPressBrake retrofit retains independent Pilz/AKAS safety authority while adding ordinary FPGA outputs, motors, solenoid switching, drives and cabinet wiring. Can a retrofit be declared safe merely because the safety input module is rated, the OSSD signals look normal at rest, and the new ordinary controller works?

**No.** The relevant question is whether the *installed cable and reference network*, in realistic operating and maintenance states, preserves the selected safety input/output semantics and physical final-element response.

Predecessors checked directly: [PWR-01](../safety-course/4400_PWR_01_SAFETY_IO_SUPPLY_RETURN_AND_DIAGNOSTIC_CONFIG_MODULE_2026-10-24.md) covers supply/return and test-pulse configuration; [BOND-01](4400_OSHA_SCHNEIDER_PROTECTIVE_BONDING_DOOR_HINGE_VARIANT_AND_EMC_BOUNDARY_AUDIT_2026-10-25.md) separates protective earth from functional shield/bond; [dependency/CCF review](SAFETY_BLOCK_DEPENDENCY_CCF_REVIEW_2026-09-24.md) identifies shared harness/EMC components; [BD63](../hardware/4000-board-design/BD63_BOARD_WIDE_POWER_DOMAIN_RETURN_CURRENT_SHIELD_CHASSIS_AND_FAULT_CONTAINMENT_CLOSURE.md) covers ordinary PCB power/return closure. **Increment:** original safety-device and drive manufacturer *installation rules* for motor/test-pulse/OSSD harness segregation, shield/reference topology, wiring replacement, and commissioning under disturbance, plus an exact physical-installation witness. Not a new safety authority, PL claim, or duplicate generic grounding lesson.

## Original-source evidence — inspected 2026-10-27
1. **DOC-CONFIRMED, original Pilz PDF:** PNOZmulti Installation Manual **1002265-EN-02**, §§3.1–3.3, 5.1, 5.6, 5.9; [original](https://www.pilz.com/download/open/PNOZmulti_Inst_Manual_1002265-EN-02.pdf). Scope names PNOZmulti, Mini and PNOZmulti 2 families; the installed OpenPressBrake Pilz type and its applicable edition are **UNKNOWN**. Pilz identifies DC/common-impedance, capacitive, inductive, radiated and static-discharge coupling. For its defined voltage groups: group 1 is <60 VDC/<25 VAC, group 2 is 60–400 VDC/25–400 VAC, group 3 >400 V supply. It recommends separate ducts or at least **10 cm** group-1/2 and **50 cm** group-1/3 separation, plus routing signal lines near earthed surfaces. These are **manufacturer installation guidance for its stated grouping**, not universal safety distances or proof of safety performance. Exact §5.1 forbids routing *test-pulse lines with actuator cables in an unprotected multicore cable*, and forbids using test outputs to drive loads. §3.3 asks for installation EMC review including operating conditions, supplies, bonds, inductive loads and mutual interference. §§3.2.4–3.2.5 distinguish equipotential bonding, braided shield, shield-bar connection, and different treatment for digital versus analogue/encoder wiring. §5.9 specifically calls for short shielded encoder adapter paths, suitable strain relief, and an isolated sensor supply in the described motion-monitoring arrangement. All product/variant specific.
2. **DOC-CONFIRMED, original SICK PDF:** deTec4 operating instructions **8021645/1SWC/2026-02-04**, §§4.4, 6.1; [original](https://cdn.sick.com/media/docs/1/41/841/operating_instructions_detec4_safety_light_curtain_en_im0079841.pdf). The two OSSDs must **not** be electrically joined and must be evaluated separately; one OFF requires a safe machine response. SICK warns that similarly shaped connectors can be misconnected and recommends a separate sheathed cable per M12 connection (with stated T-connector exception), and its §6.1 drawing shows dual-channel isolated wiring. For the stated negative-voltage-sensitive load topology, the protective device and load 0 V connections must each go separately and directly to the same 0 V terminal strip. **Do not generalize this to all OSSD devices, substitute it for PE, or assert that deTec4 is installed.** The manual's 20-ms supply-buffer requirement is a *product electrical requirement*, not a machine stop/restart time.
3. **DOC-CONFIRMED, original drive manufacturer PDF:** VACON 100 enclosed-drive installation manual **DPD01666B, dated 2015-10-06**, §5.3; [original](https://assets.danfoss.com/documents/276985/AN361179394701en-000101.pdf). It instructs crossing motor and other cables at 90°, avoiding long parallel routes, using the specified EMC shield termination, and gives **0.3 m** separation for shielded motor cable length ≤50 m, **1.0 m** for ≤200 m, including other systems' signal cables. This is a *different, high-power enclosed-drive family* (75–800 kW); not a generic minimum for a small press brake, and not evidence a VACON is installed. It shows why blindly copying Pilz's 10-cm group rule is wrong: **both selected products' actual manuals and the final installation govern**.
4. **DOC-CONFIRMED, original IEC publisher scope only:** [IEC 61000-6-7:2014](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/4254) addresses immunity claims for equipment intended for safety-related systems in industrial locations; [IEC 61326-3-1:2017](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/30986) covers applicable measurement/control/lab equipment performing safety-related functions. **Paid test levels, performance criteria and clauses not inspected**. Component EMC qualification does not validate the assembled machine's stop/hold/restart function.
5. **SOURCE-CONFIRMED (earlier pinned LinuxCNC work, not newly re-executed):** ordinary HAL/FPGA motion and diagnostic outputs are not independent personnel-safety control. **TEST-CONFIRMED:** no new test. **COMMUNITY-REPORTED:** none relied upon.

## Distinct causal/failure paths — engineering INFERENCE, not observed faults
| Initiator / shortcut | Possible deceptive symptom | Required discriminator |
|---|---|---|
| Safety input/test-pulse wire bundled with high-current coil/motor wiring | Nuisance trips, masking/bridging of diagnostic pulses, or wrong interpretation in a fault | Actual cable schedule, protective separation, exact selected test-source/receiver manuals and permitted short-fault coverage |
| Shared harness crushed or re-terminated | Both nominal channels shorted to each other or to supply; 'two wires' remain visible | Channel-specific cable/connector map and diagnostic fault assumptions, not a green PLC LED |
| 0 V reference rises or opens during inductive switching | OSSD/load state and receiving logic disagree, or output appears permissive | Selected common-reference topology, independent voltage/return verification and safe-state analysis |
| Shield drain or door hinge used as protective earth | Normal signals work while exposed metal lacks a qualified fault-clearing path | Separate PE continuity/protection qualification; BOND-01 remains authoritative |
| New VFD or solenoid cable moved into safety duct | Installed EMC assumptions change even if no schematic/netlist changes | Before/after cable routes, shield clamps, strain relief, protective barriers, operating-state review and CHG record |
| Similar M12 plugs swapped during service | Correct-looking input/LED is assigned to wrong protective zone | Keyed/labeled connectors, wiring verification against as-built, independent field-to-final-element witness |
| Repeated EMC nuisance trip | Operator bypasses safety input to keep production moving | Remove disturbance cause, clear readable diagnostics, easy duct/connector maintenance; never defeat safeguard |

Do not assume interference necessarily produces a **false safe**. Depending on exact device and circuit it may trip, remain faulted, misreport, or fail differently. Which path is credible and detectable is **UNKNOWN** without the selected hardware, wiring and failure analysis.

## Authority / witness chain
- **Ordinary control:** LinuxCNC/FPGA may request motion and log diagnostic changes. Its software 'safety healthy' indication is not a protective output or a proof of stopped ram.
- **Independent safety-related control:** selected Pilz/AKAS/sensors/receiver/test pulses own their qualified safety logic, wiring and diagnostic response; a nominal 24 V level or matching dual inputs alone is insufficient.
- **Physical energy removal/hold:** selected contactors/valves/mechanical holding devices must be independently validated for Y1/Y2 stopping and gravity-load holding; a faulted input or output-low command is not that physical witness.
- **Service isolation:** lockable electrical/hydraulic isolation, trapped pressure, and mechanical restraint remain separate from EMC commissioning or emergency stop.

## Human factors / decision
Make correct wiring the easiest: separate and clearly marked safety-signal duct, factory-terminated keyed M12 where appropriate, wide-area shield clamps, protected test-pulse routing, accessible replacement cables and diagrams, and diagnostic labels that identify the actual channel and cause. Avoid a layout where a technician must remove a guard or defeat an input to reach a connector. Never replace protective earth with a shield, or replace the independent safety controller with FPGA software.

**Information-gain stop:** product-level source question answered. ## Exact Pilz edition and cascading-cable source-depth (same session)
The original Pilz PDF back page identifies **1002265-EN-02, 2016-05**; its references to older directives/standards therefore must not be misrepresented as current normative compliance. The manufacturer's current hosting of the PDF does **not** make the manual newly published in 2026. The selected installed controller/manual revision must be identified before applying a numerical rule. Original §5.13.6 further specifies that, **outside the control cabinet**, cascading input conductors (CI+/CI-) and cascading output conductors (CO+/CO-) must be in **separate multicore cables**. This is a *specific cascade topology*, not a blanket ban on every pair of safety conductors in a multicore, and no cascade is established on OpenPressBrake. It adds a potential common-cable cross-coupling dependency to conditional EMC-2/4 only if such cascading is installed. **DOC-CONFIRMED direct**, physical applicability UNKNOWN.
