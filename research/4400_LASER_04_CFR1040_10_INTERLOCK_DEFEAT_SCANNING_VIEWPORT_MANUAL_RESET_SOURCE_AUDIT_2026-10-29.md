## High-consequence exact distinctions

1. **Accessible radiation includes reflection and operating configuration:** §1040.10(b)(15) defines human access for products containing Class IIIb/IV radiation to include access via a single introduced flat reflective surface through a housing opening. §(c)(1) classifies the *product* by highest accessible radiation during operation, not solely the installed internal source's nameplate. §(e)(2)(iii) addresses measurement in configurations involving removed housing/defeated interlocks when necessary to determine compliance. **Engineering implication (INFERENCE):** a long-stock aperture and shiny workpiece must be assessed as an optical path, not simply as a door-switch state.
2. **Protective housing and guard interlocks:** §(f)(1) sets the housing objective for access unnecessary to intended operation. §(f)(2)(i)–(iii) describes when housing portions require interlocks and when multiple interlocks or prevention of removal is required. §(f)(2)(iv) requires an audible or visible defeat indication when defeat-capable interlocks are defeated, and §(f)(2)(v) addresses replacing the housing while defeated. **Do not** turn these into instructions to defeat an interlock. They are design and inspection criteria for qualified reviewers.
3. **Product-level key/remote interlock/beam attenuator are distinct:** §(f)(3) remote-interlock connector, §(f)(4) removable key master control for Class IIIb/IV systems, §(f)(6) separate beam-attenuator provision for Class II–IV (subject to stated alternate approval). A key or remote connector is **not** proof of complete emission inhibit, maintenance lockout, guarded personnel access, or verified final-element response. The actual laser class and exceptions control applicability.
4. **Viewing optics and scanner failures:** §(f)(8) applies accessible radiation limits through integrated viewing optics, viewports and displays, including shutter/attenuator failure protection; §(f)(9) addresses failure of scan velocity or amplitude for products emitting accessible scanned radiation. **INFERENCE:** a galvo scanner stuck on one spot or a substituted clear viewport is a different optical exposure failure from ordinary CNC axis following error. Do not invent a scan-fault reaction time or laser-power threshold from the regulatory overview.
5. **Fresh manual reset:** §(f)(10) specifies a manual reset for Class IV laser systems manufactured after 1986-08-20 following a remote-interlock interruption or unexpected main-power loss exceeding five seconds. This is a **specific product-scope regulatory trigger**, not a universal five-second safety design rule. For other modes, machinery and worksite controls, determine separate appropriate deliberate restart conditions from risk assessment and adopted standards.
6. **Indicators:** §(f)(5) includes emission indication and visibility through protective eyewear in its defined scope. A ready lamp or CNC HMI bit does not prove safe optical containment or independent interlock integrity.

## Failure-path desk review, no energy

| Paper scenario | Fault hypothesis | Discriminator and required witness |
|---|---|---|
| Enclosed laser with pass-through | reflected beam exits aperture despite 'Class 1' label | exact product/mode classification, aperture and reflection analysis, qualified accessible-emission evidence |
| One interlock stuck made | single failure opens access to high radiation | exact housing removal constraints, qualified redundant/physical prevention architecture, source-inhibit final element and independent validation |
| Defeat-capable service switch left active | normal panel replacement hides bypass | qualified mode/defeat indication, physical prevention/reinstatement and deliberate recommissioning; no live defeat instructed |
| Galvo scan motion stalls | dwell concentration or accessible scanned radiation changes | OEM scanner safeguards and independent optical emission inhibit; no claim that LinuxCNC velocity feedback proves optical safety |
| Power returns after interruption | emission resumes unexpectedly | exact product class/date and §(f)(10) trigger, plus independent fresh start/rearm per actual risk assessment |
| Ordinary clear viewport replaces rated part | visible image remains but invisible radiation passes | exact OEM filter spectral rating, replacement identity and qualified inspection; no operation until resolved |
