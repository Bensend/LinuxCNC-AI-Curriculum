## Breadth conclusions

1. Plasma is the most mature LinuxCNC process-cutting specialization because QtPlasmaC + `plasmac.comp` integrate height sensing, arc readiness, THC, recovery, materials and process HMI.
2. Laser has first-class realtime building blocks in the pinned tree (`laserpower.comp`, `raster.comp`, `configs/sim/axis/laser`) but a thinner integrated production workflow.
3. Waterjet has real LinuxCNC retrofit/use evidence but no upstream dedicated process controller comparable to QtPlasmaC found in this bounded search. Treat that as an implementation-research gap, not proof none exists.
4. The three processes share gantry motion and synchronized I/O but must keep process command, readiness, feedback/height loop, CAM recipe and recovery semantics separate.
5. No lab was launched from breadth alone; next information gain is source/config/build-diary tracing.

## Exact next work

### 1. 3300-P1 — QtPlasmaC / plasmac source trace

Trace the actual process state machine from pinned source:

- initial height sensing / probe;
- pierce height;
- torch request;
- Arc OK / transfer;
- pierce delay / puddle jump where applicable;
- cut-height transition;
- THC enable/disable qualification;
- cut end/retract;
- arc-start failure;
- arc loss;
- breakaway/probe faults;
- pause, abort and cut-recovery behavior.

Map exactly where external Z offsets enter the motion path and how they are reset/limited.

### 2. 3300-P2 — real plasma implementations

Inspect at least two real QtPlasmaC/PlasmaC machine configurations or build diaries from beginning through later faults/changes.

Prefer variation:

- THCAD arc-voltage + Arc OK;
- ohmic + float fallback;
- a Powermax RS485 machine if inspectable;
- one machine with tandem gantry/squaring or another nontrivial physical integration.

Record commissioning failures such as reversed/scaled arc voltage, false Arc OK, noisy probing, torch breakaway and postprocessor mistakes.

### 3. 3300-P3 — plasma production workflow

Inspect:

- material file authority/provenance;
- SheetCam/Fusion/other QtPlasmaC post behavior;
- M190/material selection;
- M62/M63 THC inhibit;
- M67 velocity reduction;
- hole/overcut strategy;
- run-from-line/cut recovery;
- consumable state and HMI diagnostics.

Keep CAM-generated intent separate from realtime process-control authority.

## Candidate labs only if source leaves a gap

- plasma external-offset cleanup on abort;
- stale Arc OK or probe signal across a new pierce request;
- laser M67/M62 queued output with no following motion;
- laser power response through controlled deceleration/cornering;
- raster pixel/position boundary behavior;
- water/abrasive lead-lag recovery once a real public contract is known.

## Do not infer

- Do not copy plasma THC behavior into laser focus control or waterjet standoff without evidence.
- Do not treat process-ready telemetry as functional-safety authority.
- Do not equate a CAM material/tool record with the controller's currently active and physically valid process state.
- Do not assume a queued synchronized output has executed without the motion event that commits it.
- Do not present QtPlasmaC conversational tools as a full general-purpose CAM system.
- Do not claim LinuxCNC has a standard waterjet process state machine unless inspectable source/config evidence is found.
- Do not treat five configured axes as proof of production-quality bevel/taper compensation; kinematics, process physics and postprocessing remain separate.
