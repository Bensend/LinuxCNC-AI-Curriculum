Frozen findings:
- Stopped, control-disabled, safety-stopped, energy-isolated, stored-energy-safe, and verified are separate claims.
- Hazardous stored energy may be dissipated or positively restrained; a restrained gravity load is not literally zero energy.
- Reaccumulation requires continued verification or an architecture that prevents hazardous return.
- Software status, HAL bits, ordinary FPGA state, drive-ready state, and one-time sensor readings are not automatically independent proof of physical isolation.
- Energized maintenance must be named as such and requires a task-specific alternate-protection argument.
- Machine-specific thresholds, discharge/coast times, hydraulic truth tables, support loads, and similar physical facts remain UNKNOWN until sourced/measured/engineered.
