# 4400-BLK-01 — Technician decision card: support, isolation and positioning
**2026-10-24 | 16 open-book, non-blind PAPER scenarios | NOT A PHYSICAL TEST**

For each case write: **hazardous physical proposition**, normal/safety/isolator/support authority, the strongest legitimate witness, what remains UNKNOWN, and whether personnel may enter the zone. Use [BLK-01](4400_BLK_01_GRAVITY_RESTRAINT_DYNAMIC_FORCE_AND_LOTO_MODULE_2026-10-24.md) and [original OSHA source audit](../research/4400_OSHA_SLIDE_LOCK_GRAVITY_VS_POWERED_STROKE_AND_PRESS_BRAKE_TRANSFER_AUDIT_2026-10-24.md). Never assume an unspecified device rating or hydraulic circuit.

1. Press ram is stationary; hydraulic pump is OFF; no support installed. May a worker reach beneath it?
2. A pin sensor reports ENGAGED, but the pin cannot be observed and its last inspection is unknown. What is proved?
3. The pin is visibly through a hole, but no frame anchorage calculation or rating is available. What remains unproved?
4. A support was rated for an older upper die; new tooling is heavier. Which evidence must be invalidated?
5. An E-stop is active and the safety relay output is low; a leaking cylinder can still lower the ram. Which function is missing?
6. The support is designed to withstand static gravity load; the hydraulic pump remains powered. Can the support be credited for a powered stroke?
7. A controller has two redundant inputs monitoring one physical support. Does that create two independent mechanical load paths?
8. A qualified mechanic wants one powered repositioning move mid-repair. State the required 1910.147(f)(1) transition and people-clear boundary.
9. A worker finishes repositioning and returns to the die space without reapplying LOTO. What was skipped?
10. Two maintenance workers share a lockbox, but one has no personal lock. What group-control question must be resolved?
11. A block is so heavy that staff leave it in storage and instead use 'slow jog'. Which human-factor design failure should be corrected?
12. A support pin has been bent by a prior unintended stroke. Can it be reused on the basis of a green indicator?
13. A selected machine uses a lockable hydraulic line valve but has a trapped cylinder volume and elevated load. What additional physical propositions remain?
14. A supplier claims the OSHA 2007 slide-lock appendix 'requires 2.0 on all press brakes'. Explain the scope and why that is an invalid universal approval.
15. A press-brake builder cites 1910.217(d)(9)(iv) as a binding press-brake clause. Correct the scope and identify the applicable general guarding/LOTO routes.
16. A guard is reinstalled after maintenance, but LinuxCNC retained a pending cycle command. Which reset/rearm and fresh-start tests are necessary before release?

**Scoring discipline:** No answer key or external grade in this card. Record alternatives, falsifying evidence and confidence; do not count a self-scored answer as a blind external evaluation. A claimed machine PASS without qualified physical evidence is a critical safety error.
