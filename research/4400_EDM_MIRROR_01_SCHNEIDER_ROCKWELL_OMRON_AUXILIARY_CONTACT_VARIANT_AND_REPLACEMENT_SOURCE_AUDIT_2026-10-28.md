# 4400-EDM-MIRROR-01 — The wrong NC contact can make a safety feedback loop lie
**2026-10-28 | 4000 safety-course exact-OEM source-depth | NON-GRADUATED | NOT A SELECTED-MACHINE VALIDATION**

## Question, predecessor and novelty boundary
A replacement contactor has the correct coil voltage, power poles and a spare 21–22 NC contact. May a technician reuse that NC for external-device monitoring (EDM), declare the circuit equivalent and reset a press brake? **Not without evidence of the exact contactor and exact auxiliary contact's mirror performance, mechanical mounting, wiring, feedback diagnostics and machine-level final element.**

This is **new exact manufacturer/product-variant evidence and a replacement-change discriminator**, not a new invention of EDM. Predecessors already establish reset/rearm, welded contactor, and feedback's narrow scope: [RST-01 original Pilz/Rockwell audit](4400_RESET_POWER_RETURN_EDM_WELDED_CONTACTOR_OEM_SOURCE_AUDIT_2026-10-24.md), [RST-01 learner module](../safety-course/4400_RST_01_POWER_RETURN_RESET_EDM_AND_FRESH_START_MODULE_2026-10-24.md), [2540 entry](../safety-course/2540_ENTRY_MAP_AND_RELEASE_GATE_2026-09-23.md), and [SO output template](../hardware/SO_FINAL_ELEMENT_IMPLEMENTATION_SPEC_TEMPLATE.md). This audit adds the **NC accessory variant, one-way versus two-way contact semantics, factory-retained versus side-mounted replacement, and a manufacturer certificate's exact use conditions**. Do not add another unscored exam bank.

## Causal model and asymmetric contact truth
**DOC-CONFIRMED (Schneider/Rockwell/Omron):** The qualified **NC mirror** contact is constrained **not to be closed while any associated main NO power contact is closed**, under the manufacturer's defined conditions. Therefore a welded main pole is intended to keep the NC mirror feedback from falsely returning closed when the coil drops. **It does not mean** an open NC proves the main poles are energized, that an ordinary NC accessory is a mirror contact, or that a mirror contact alone establishes an entire safety function.

**DOC-CONFIRMED (Schneider/Omron):** For **mechanically linked** NO/NC relay contact sets the manufacturer also describes the reverse incompatibility when the NC welds. Do **not** assume a contactor's one-way mirror property has the same converse guarantee. Some exact devices may be qualified to both specifications, but only if that selected product/assembly documentation says so.

| Failure or substitution | What an ordinary status bit might say | What the qualified safety argument needs |
|---|---|---|
| Main pole welded; coil off | 'Coil off' or 'EDM ready' | Confirm correct mirror NC physically associated with that power contactor; independent actual energy interruption, not coil status. |
| Replacement has a generic NC block | '21–22 continuity works' | Exact base SKU, accessory SKU, manufacturer's mirror declaration, approved mounting, wiring and validation. NC continuity alone is insufficient. |
| Side-mounted auxiliary removed, loose, mis-mounted or swapped | 'NC feedback changes in normal cycling' | Manufacturer-approved mounting and function; Suva 2022 explicitly requires testing side-mounted installation/function. |
| NC feedback wire shorted or bridged | 'EDM healthy' | Circuit diagnostics, fault masking, actual contact identification and physical independent witness. |
| NC mirror open | 'Contactor definitely energized' | Not logically implied: open may mean energized, weld, wire break, failed accessory or other fault. Treat as a fault, not proof of one physical state. |
| Two contactors and only one monitored correctly | 'Dual-channel safe' | Exact mapping K1/K2 and common supply/connector/feedback failure analysis; no redundancy claim from a wiring label. |
| Correct EDM after stop | 'Ram held safely' | Separate motor power/drive, hydraulic final elements, Y1/Y2 gravity restraint, stored energy and physical stop/hold evidence. |

## Human-factors / repair design
A safer repair should be easier than a jumper: stock **exact approved contactor + auxiliary kits**, preserve a visible photo/part ID and keyed/retained assembly, keep feedback terminals individually labeled **K1-EDM / K2-EDM**, use the manufacturer's approved mounting and replacement procedure, and provide a fault display that distinguishes **'feedback mismatch — do not reset'** from ordinary LinuxCNC servo faults. A hurried maintainer replacing a damaged side-mounted block with a mechanically compatible generic NC is a foreseeable common failure. Do not use an HMI green light, 21–22 terminal numbering, red housing, or generic 'safety' branding as sole evidence.

Do not encourage live welding of contacts, defeating EDM, forcing a contactor closed, or testing with people exposed. Approved fault-injection/validation must be designed by qualified personnel with power isolated or remotely controlled and people outside the danger zone.

## Traceable implementation and physical witness
- **AUTH/ARC:** independent safety logic owns demand/reset/EDM; LinuxCNC/HAL/ordinary FPGA may log diagnostics and request normal motion only. The selected final-element wiring and energy path remain machine-specific.
- **DEP/CCF:** selected contactor and *each* NC auxiliary SKU; physical retention/position; coil and main-pole wiring; independent feedback route; 24 V/0 V; shared connectors; bypass/test links; common motor/drive/hydraulic energy path.
- **SRS/PHY:** define exactly what power interruption contributes to the physical safe state; a press-brake ram may remain hazardous from gravity/pressure even when pump contactor poles open.
- **VAL:** document selected manufacturer certificate/edition, assembly inspection, isolated continuity/function evidence, fault-monitoring behavior, qualified independent output/physical state, power return and fresh-start behavior. Exact stop times/PL/PFHd/B10d/diagnostic coverage remain **UNKNOWN** without selected design and calculation/measurement.
- **CHG:** replacing base contactor, front/side accessory, safety relay input, terminal mapping, feedback wiring or configuration invalidates prior affected EDM validation until reviewed. Do not automatically inherit old validation because terminal numbers match.
- **Restricted Stage A:** collect exact K1/K2 and accessory markings, photos and schematics in qualified nonpublic evidence; public course retains only generic witness definitions.
