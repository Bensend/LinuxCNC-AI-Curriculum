## Four distinct sources of 'speed' that must never be silently merged

1. **Normal CNC commanded velocity:** a LinuxCNC/FPGA target, useful for ordinary motion and logging; not an independent physical speed observation.
2. **Ordinary ATEK measured Y1/Y2 position:** potentially useful normal feedback if the scales/wiring are correct; their measured differences or derived speed do not become safety-rated merely because there are two axes. The same replacement board, ordinary acquisition, power and software can be common-cause paths.
3. **Qualified safety speed/position source:** exact installed independent sensor(s), monitoring device, diagnostic coverage and safety PLC program **UNKNOWN**. The manufacturer AMS3 example illustrates a *possible F-family* two-sensor architecture, not the installed machine.
4. **Physical stop and gravity-load hold:** actual Y1/Y2 stopping and retention, hydraulic final elements, load support and pressure/energy must be separately witnessed. Even a valid safety-speed signal cannot establish this physical outcome.

**INFERENCE failure hypothesis:** a CNC output could assert MUTE based on ordinary ATEK-derived 'slow' while the retained safety program fails to independently reject a stuck request, failed sensor, axis disagreement, or stale position. Whether the real Pilz program prevents this is **UNKNOWN**; this document does not assert such a defect exists.
