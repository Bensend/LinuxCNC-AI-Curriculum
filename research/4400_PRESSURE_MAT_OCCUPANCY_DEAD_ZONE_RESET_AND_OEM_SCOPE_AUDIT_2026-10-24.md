# 4400 source audit — pressure-sensitive safety mats: occupancy, joints, reset and false confidence
**2026-10-24 | Status: documentary source reading, NOT GRADUATED, NO MACHINE TEST.** This is a different protective function from the tactile *edge* and emergency cable-pull investigations. No claim that OpenPressBrake has a mat.

## Question and decision
Can a pressure-sensitive floor mat independently establish that a rear machine zone is unoccupied, that a new machine start is deliberate, or that a press-brake ram is physically stopped and held? **No, not by its catalog name, indicator or nominal response time.** A correctly engineered mat may contribute to presence detection in a defined floor area, but its sensing footprint, wiring, controller, reset path and downstream physical final elements must be separately evidenced. Never allocate personnel-safety decisions solely to LinuxCNC HAL, the normal FPGA or HMI.

## Three non-interchangeable protection claims
**Approach/trip:** a person crosses an effective sensing footprint far enough from the hazard that the *complete* protective function stops before reach. **Occupied-zone prevention:** a person already within a guarded cell continues to actuate a mat while present, and the independent safety logic prevents rearm/start. **Physical hazard elimination:** the actual moving element has stopped and is restrained against gravity or stored energy. One can fail while another appears healthy. An emergency-stop cable, tactile contact edge, perimeter light curtain and floor mat are not substitutes without risk-based functional analysis.

A mat **cannot guarantee full-body occupancy** where someone can stand on a non-sensing surface, climb on the machine, sit on a workpiece, be lifted, or occupy an area outside its actual footprint. Conversely, a large mat may nuisance-trip during legitimate setup; redesign the workflow/coverage so protection is practical instead of encouraging defeat.

## Deliberately adversarial source reconciliation
- **35 kg versus 20 kg:** ISO scope describes possible device classes, not automatic detection capability of a 35 kg Rockwell product. Do not assume a 20 kg person is detected.
- **35 ms versus whole stop:** Rockwell's 35 ms is mat/controller response **t1**, not hydraulic ram stopping/holding. Never copy the example 2032 mm separation distance.
- **Legacy formula versus current standard:** 2024 OEM manuals quote an earlier ISO 13855 formula. **ISO 13855:2024** must be reviewed in full and the machine-specific Type-C standard applied before any numeric acceptance. No current-edition numerical result is claimed here.
- **Product family versus exact variant:** Rockwell 440F-C4000P/D and Schmersal SMS4/5 have different terminal/evaluator and reset contexts. A common 'four-wire' label does not establish plug compatibility.
- **Green versus safe:** machine-enabled LEDs and ordinary HMI tags do not prove physically held hydraulic axes, absence of a trapped person or independent final-element authority.

## Human factors and practical design choices
Use flush, mechanically secured, non-slip transitions with joints that are easy to inspect and replace. Route cables in protected channels with accessible service loops; keep spare trim and documented compatible mats available. Make the operator's legitimate access and material handling feasible **without** standing on/bridging a safeguard. If bypass is easier than normal work, redesign the geometry and task before commissioning. Reset should have meaningful visibility or a verified occupancy-clearing method and must not itself initiate hazardous motion. Display mat/zone fault identity in LinuxCNC for diagnostics **only**; safety authority stays in the independently qualified chain.

## Qualified verification plan — NOT EXECUTED
1. Freeze selected hazard geometry/modes and all possible approach/standing locations; determine whether a mat is even appropriate. Photograph/journal floor-level seams, stock routes, removable platforms and raised access. Keep private machine drawings out of this public repo.
2. Record exact mat and controller catalog numbers, manuals/revisions, connector/cable, series/parallel topology, monitored fault behavior, output class, reset mode and physical final elements. Trace independent safety power/return.
3. With machine motion **isolated and made incapable of hazardous operation**, qualified personnel can verify sensing coverage and monitor/controller logic using the OEM commissioning sequence. Never use a person as a live hazardous-motion test target.
4. Independently validate worst-case physical stopping, load holding, residual energy, occupancy and deliberate fresh start under a separately approved remote/isolated plan. No values or fault injections are supplied here.
5. Revalidate after mats, trim, floor coverings, machine speed, hydraulics, software mode, controller, cable, reset location or material-handling geometry change.
