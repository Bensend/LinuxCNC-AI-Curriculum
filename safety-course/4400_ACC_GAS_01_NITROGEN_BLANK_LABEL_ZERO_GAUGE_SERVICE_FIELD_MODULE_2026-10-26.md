# ACC-GAS-01 field module — Zero on the oil gauge is not an empty pressure vessel
**Status: PUBLIC PAPER ONLY / UNSCORED / NOT RUN / NON-GRADUATED.** Prerequisite: 2520 hazardous energy, 2580 hydraulic safety, 4400 SAF gauge-zone boundary. [Manufacturer source audit](../research/4400_ACC_GAS_01_HYDAC_PARKER_GAS_SIDE_RESIDUAL_ENERGY_AND_BLANK_PRECHARGE_SOURCE_AUDIT_2026-10-26.md).

## Learning outcome
Explain why **hydraulic gauge = zero**, **pump = off**, **STO = active**, **blank nameplate precharge**, **gas-side verified empty**, **gravity load supported**, and **maintenance authorized** are seven distinct propositions. Do not conflate normal control, diagnostics, independent safety, or physical restraint. This module authorizes **no physical valve manipulation, pressure test or live machine work**.

## Ten public technician transfer prompts — all UNSCORED
1. Pump off, HMI dark, hydraulic gauge zero. What physical facts remain unproven before touching an accumulator?
2. Parker BA nameplate precharge field blank. What does the original manual say about possible holding charge? Is the exact selected unit proven charged?
3. HYDAC SAF oil-side gauge suddenly falls to zero during the manufacturer's described precharge check. Which **preceding** reading matters and why is this not proof of gas-side zero?
4. Pressure transducer shows zero but the isolating cock to the sensor is closed. What does that reading prove about the vessel?
5. Maintenance technician proposes replacing a gas-valve core with an automotive tire valve. Which manufacturer boundary applies?
6. A spare accumulator arrives with a capped gas port. What must be established before transporting, lifting or disassembling it?
7. A technician sees fluid at the gas valve. What product-specific failure hypothesis and stop-work escalation follow?
8. Two accumulators are on separate branches; one gauge is near the pump. What diagram and port evidence are missing?
9. A new accumulator has a different gas valve and pressure rating. Which previous commissioning/maintenance evidence becomes stale?
10. Operator wants to bypass a guard to read the vessel nameplate. Redesign access so the correct path is easier.

For each response, require: **hazard**, **what is actually known**, **what is not proved**, **who has authority**, **safe next documentary/qualified action**, and **what would trigger HOLD**. Never reward a numeric guessed precharge, improvised venting, or unauthorized hands-on demonstration.
