## Distinct question and predecessor
Why can two shipped INIs with the same `TRAJ_PERIOD` produce different motion module parameters? Trace actual HAL substitution, period quantization and diagnostics. This is **not** a novel INI parser bug; it is a concrete machine-configuration ownership and source provenance exercise. Prior HAL-19/20 and PLASMA-33 teach thread ordering but not this exact parameter path. Direct PROGRESS and research filename scans found no previous `traj_period_nsec` audit.

## Trace and discriminators
`[EMCMOT]TRAJ_PERIOD` → **only if HAL passes it** `loadrt motmod traj_period_nsec=...` → `motion.c` defaults/rounds periods → HAL `thread->period` nominal callback → first `emcmotController` recalculation → TP/cubic internal fields → joint commands. The **same INI** may yield different effective motmod parameters when a different HAL file is used.

| Observable | Owner | Interpretation | Not proof of |
|---|---|---|---|
| INI SERVO/TRAJ | Author | Requested values | Passed motmod parameters |
| HAL loadrt substitution | HAL author | Parameters passed | Actual thread quantization |
| `thread->period` | HAL scheduler | Nominal callback interval | Wakeup jitter |
| `motion.servo.last-period[-ns]` | Motion callback | Measured interval (revision-specific units) | Nominal period or encoder timestamp |
| `emcmotConfig->servoCycleTime` | Motion setters | Internal config field | Actual HAL callback period when RT-133 mismatch exists |
| TP cycle / cubic rate/segment | TP/cubic | Internal command-generation timing | Physical motion or safety |

**Source-derived rounding discriminator:** BASE=400,000 ns and SERVO=1,000,000 ns yields a requested servo/base ratio of 2.5, rounded by `int(ratio+0.5)` to 3, i.e. nominal 1,200,000 ns *if the HAL clock honors the requested base*. Omitted traj retains its default requested 1,000,000 ns and first callback reaches RT-133's zero quotient. Additional RTAPI clock rounding or earlier thread creation may change actual values.
