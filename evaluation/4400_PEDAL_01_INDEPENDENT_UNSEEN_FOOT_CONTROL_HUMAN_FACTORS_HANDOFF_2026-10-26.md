# 4400-PEDAL-01 — Information-separated evaluator handoff

**2026-10-26. PREPARED / NOT RUN.** This public file is a *handoff specification*, not a hidden answer key, blind challenge, learner response or score.

## Competency to assess

On a previously unseen industrial foot-control installation, the learner must distinguish command initiation, anti-trip mechanical design, ergonomic defeat incentives, separate hazard-zone protection, independent stopping/holding, and restart authority. Test exact manufacturer/guard identity and OSHA 1910.217 press-brake exclusion rather than accepting product-family generalizations.

## Evaluator preparation (keep unseen details separate)

An independent evaluator should choose a **different** exact foot-switch/guard model and application than the public 511-B4OXG/SightGuard example. Independently retain source/manual revision, mechanical actuation and return sequence, actual switch topology, footwear and nearby object routes, cable/relocation constraints, control/SRS boundary, and any known machine-specific witness limitations. Do not reveal an answer, scored resolution, or grading notes to the learner before immutable precommitment. Do not invent a PL/SIL, hydraulic truth table or measured stop distance.

Provide only a bounded initial symptom/evidence packet and allowed resources. Ask for (1) predicted false-start or missed-start mechanism, (2) discriminating no-energy checks, (3) the easiest defensible redesign, (4) what remains UNKNOWN about point-of-operation guarding, final elements and restart, (5) alternatives/falsifier, (6) confidence and time.

## Integrity and scoring

Use [BLIND_FEEDBACK_PROTOCOL](BLIND_FEEDBACK_PROTOCOL.md): score prediction, mechanism, diagnostic efficiency, uncertainty/safety and confidence calibration (0–2 each, total /10) only **after** learner response is durably precommitted. Classify misses; use a novel same-mechanism transfer and delayed retention when justified. Do not add a score to [FEEDBACK_SCORE_LOG](FEEDBACK_SCORE_LOG.md) until a valid independent run exists. If evaluator has seen the learner's answer key or the challenge is near-verbatim, mark **NON-BLIND/INVALID** and do not count it.
