# 4400 electrical point-of-work verification — NFPA committee response, vendor conflict and AVT installation scope
**2026-10-24 | Original source-reading audit | Not machine acceptance, not a legal determination**

## Question and frozen engineering boundary
Can a cabinet-door voltage-presence lamp, high-impedance external test point or remotely networked voltage bit justify opening a CNC enclosure for maintenance? **No universal equivalence is established.** Identify and lock out all energy sources, and have a qualified person verify the actual exposed parts. A correctly selected, installed and commissioned absence-of-voltage tester (AVT) may perform its narrowly specified voltage-verification function under the applicable standard/procedure; it does not isolate energy, prove all panel circuits safe, or validate hydraulic/gravity holding. **Dark voltage-presence indicator does not mean zero voltage.**

## Original primary-source evidence, edition and scope
1. **OSHA 29 CFR 1910.333(a)(1), (b)(2)(ii)(B), (b)(2)(iv)(A–B)**: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.333 . **DOC-CONFIRMED**: deenergize exposed live parts subject to stated exceptions; disconnect all electrical sources; pushbuttons, selector switches and interlocks cannot serve as sole deenergization; a qualified person checks inability to restart and tests exposed parts including induced voltage and unrelated backfeed. The **over 600 V nominal** before/after tester-operation clause is explicitly in OSHA 1910.333; do not imply it limits other applicable live-dead-live best practice.
2. **OSHA 1910.147(b), (d)(3–6), (f)** https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.147 . **DOC-CONFIRMED**: control circuit switches are not energy-isolating devices; stored and reaccumulating energy and isolation require verification; temporary energization for testing has separate procedures. OSHA 1910.303(g) https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.303 and 1910.335 https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.335 address guarded live parts, working space, PPE and barriers. Actual applicability is task/jurisdiction dependent.
3. **NFPA EEW-AAA technical committee, August 19–21 2025 second-draft meeting minutes**, original **340-page PDF p.104 (zero-based p.103 visually inspected)** https://docinfofiles.nfpa.org/files/AboutTheCodes/70E/70E_A2026_EEW_AAA_SD_Minutes_0825.pdf . **DOC-CONFIRMED committee-document wording, NOT a published formal interpretation or proven final adopted standard text.** The committee proposed response to 120.6(7) says permanently mounted equipment used to test absence of voltage, including permanently mounted devices used with portable test instruments, must meet Exception No. 1 to item 7. Other pages reproduce AVT exception requirements: listed/labeled for absence testing, installed at actual point of work, phase-to-phase and phase-to-ground, known-source functionality before/after. Minutes contain proposed/struck wording; do not silently promote drafts to adopted requirements.
4. **Earlier contrary vendor interpretation:** Grace Technologies authors writing in Rockwell's Journal, in advance of NFPA 70E 2024, argue UL 61010 high-impedance external test points with portable meters can meet 120.6(7) without the AVT exception, citing earlier 2021 committee public-input response: https://www.rockwellautomation.com/en-us/company/news/the-journal/use-point-of-work-electrical-safety-devices.html . **DOC-CONFIRMED as vendor claim, not current authoritative standard determination.** Practical conclusion conflicts with the later 2025 committee proposed response. **UNKNOWN**: current final NFPA text, any separately issued formal interpretation, AHJ adoption and particular device qualification. Get authoritative adopted language and qualified review before crediting external test points as formal absence proof.
5. **Panduit VeriSafe 2.0 VS2-AVT B21110 Rev.4, October 6 2022**, original **20-page OEM PDF** https://www.panduit.com/content/dam/panduit/en/website/landing-pages/pdf/vs2-avt-instruction-manual-1002985--b21110-en-rev4.pdf . **DOC-CONFIRMED only for this exact OEM/model/version:** PDF p.3: AVT addresses only voltage-absence step and only sensor termination location; no red lamps alone cannot prove absence. PDF pp.12–13: choose actual test point, label it, no circuit elements between paired sensor leads; multiple sources can need multiple AVTs. PDF pp.3,7: high-frequency VFD-output sections unsuitable; sensor lead and EMI limits model-specific. PDF p.15: repeat and record full commissioning **after electrical changes**, including deenergized/energized/deenergized checks; green must not illuminate when energized. PDF pp.16–17: active self-test, known-voltage check, phase-to-phase and ground tests, lead-contact verification; four caution flashes means lead verification failed, five to eight means hardware failure. A failed/no-response test, dark red indicator, remote dashboard or green at another point is not proof for exposed parts.
6. **Panduit vendor article February 5 2026** https://www.panduit.com/emea/en/about/blog/nfpa-70e-technical-committee.html calls the committee action a formal interpretation. **Provenance caution:** original artifact examined is second-draft meeting minutes with a proposed response; no separate formal interpretation instrument was independently established.

## Failure-path analysis
| Failure / shortcut | Mechanism and required discriminator |
|---|---|
| Main disconnect OFF but auxiliary supply energized | Other feed bypasses isolator; map/lock/test every source at actual exposed point. |
| Green AVT upstream, shared DC link charged downstream | AVT measures only sensor location; independently verify DC bus and other feeds. |
| Red presence LEDs dark; no active test | Darkness is not absence proof; require qualified approved absence procedure. |
| Retrofit mechanically bridges paired AVT sensor leads | OEM connection test can be defeated; reject and re-commission. |
| AVT relocated to VFD PWM output | OEM warns high-frequency/EMI application unsuitable; no transfer. |
| Test-point vendor claims universal current NFPA compliance | Original 2025 committee proposed response conflicts with earlier vendor article; resolve final adopted standard. |
| LinuxCNC E-stop or drive STO treated as lockable isolation | Motion stop is not maintenance energy isolation. |
| Door interlock defeated for recurring troubleshooting | Redesign external protected diagnostics and service access; bypass incentive is a design defect. |

## Separate propositions
**Normal LinuxCNC/FPGA control** may request stops and display alarms; **monitoring** may display voltage presence; **independent safety-related control** may remove hazardous motion commands; **physical isolation and qualified verification** establish specific electrical maintenance conditions; **mechanical/hydraulic mechanisms** address gravity and pressure separately. None substitutes for the others.
