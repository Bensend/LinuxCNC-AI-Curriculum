# 4400 — ISO 14119 edition identity (2026-10-21)

**DOC-CONFIRMED, catalog scope only.** ISO lists ISO 14119:2024, edition 3, published 2024-09-10, as the current international standard on interlocking devices and foreseeable defeat: https://www.iso.org/standard/75942.html

DIN Media lists DIN EN ISO 14119:2025-09 as the German version of EN ISO 14119:2025, based on ISO 14119:2024: https://www.dinmedia.de/en/standard/din-en-iso-14119/390072907

BSI lists BS EN ISO 14119:2025+A11:2026 as current, published 2026-06-30: https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/products/safety-of-machinery-interlocking-devices-associated-with-guards-principles-for-design-and-selection-5

Pilz's July 2025 guard-locking whitepaper refers to EN ISO 14119:2025 Table J.1 when discussing fault masking in mechanically series-connected contacts: https://www.pilz.com/download/open/WPaper_Whitepaper_guard_locking_1007349-EN-01.pdf

**UNKNOWN:** applicability of the A11 amendment and any numerical diagnostic-coverage result; full licensed standard clauses were not inspected. Do not conflate international ISO edition year with EN national adoption year, or claim a machine's safety level from this edition register. No physical test or GitHub-hosted compute was performed.
