# 4400-MAG-LIFT-01 — Permanent magnetic hold survives power loss, but not an unqualified load or release command

## Question / novelty
Does an emergency stop that removes **electrical or compressed-air switching energy** release a magnetic lifting attachment, and does a vendor's power-failure retention claim prove a particular thin sheet can be lifted safely? Full canonical safety-index scan found no prior lifting-magnet retention / magnetic WLL branch. Existing STO/holding, 2580 stored energy, crane and VAC-LIFT-01 drop-zone reasoning are predecessors; **different physical holding principle and release hazard**, not a new scored safety competency.

## Failure-path table (SOURCE/TEST claims not upgraded)
| Event | Permanent-magnet product claim | Remaining physical risk / missing witness |
|---|---|---|
| E-stop removes control power while magnet already ON | Optimag E and Permatrol claim retained magnetic state | Sheet slips due to thin stock, coating, gap, oil, edge lifting, shock, deformation or failed attachment; no qualified real WLL/geometry |
| E-stop dumps pneumatic air after Optimag P ON | OEM claims permanent hold remains | A previously OFF unit remains OFF; an unintended release pulse/incorrect valve sequence or mechanical detachment is not excluded by this claim |
| Controller lamp says FULL MAG | Manufacturer documents current-sensing interlock | Sensed switching current is **not** measured payload holding force, all poles' contact, actual workpiece presence or safe clearance |
| Load is supported, operator requests release | Switching power/air may deliberately demagnetize | Release should be inhibited unless independently proven load is landed/supported and danger zone clear; actual controller interlock is model/configuration-dependent |
| Maintenance after disconnect | Magnetic field can remain without electrical/air power | Electrical LOTO does **not** remove permanent magnetic attraction or suspended-load hazard; positive support and qualified release procedure needed |
| Payload material changes from thick clean steel to thin/painted/perforated | OEM warns air gap/thickness/permeability derate | Earlier lift/witness becomes **STALE**; no numerical downrate or permitted load can be inferred without exact OEM evidence |

**INFERENCE:** A power-loss-retaining magnet can prevent one drop scenario while creating a different residual-energy/service trap: removing supply power does not demagnetize or make a suspended part safe to approach. Unlike an ordinary energized electromagnet, these specific products have bistable permanent-magnet holding; do not generalize their behavior to every magnetic lifter or assume safety integrity from a product's word “failsafe.” The independent safety system must address robot/hoist motion, guarding, payload retention, support-before-release and deliberate restart; LinuxCNC/FPGA may request/monitor but cannot be the sole personnel-safety authority.

## Minimum safe engineering sequence (conditional)
1. Select **actual** magnet model, exact sheet/part, thickness, permeability, surface, air gap, temperature, orientation, dynamic loads, number of poles, attachment and hoist rating. Obtain complete current manual, configuration, OEM model-specific WLL and written resolution of the E **600/400 kg** scope conflict if relevant. Do not invent safe force.
2. Derive independent safety functions for hazardous motion stop, load support/retention on supply loss, release interlock, guard/exclusion, maintenance isolation and new-start behavior. Identify shared controller/power/cable and accidental release paths. Never count “current sensor OK” as proof of contact force.
3. Favor simple physical means: guarded drop zone, mechanically supported landing position, load-supported-before-release interlock, clear operator feedback, easy safe retrieval, captive guards. No person should need to enter a drop zone to clear a fault or rearm.
4. **HOLD:** without qualified physical holding and drop-zone exclusion, no personnel-exposed operation. Do not execute deliberate power-loss, release, load-drop or rated-load experiments from this curriculum. Any future bounded validation requires qualified owner approval, isolated/remote setup and people outside the hazard.
5. After tool/part/finish/air gap/safety logic change, mark WLL/holding/guard/release evidence STALE. Revalidate before people are exposed.

## Same-session real industrial implementation — independent release intent versus actual supported load

**DOC-CONFIRMED directly opened original manufacturer product page**: Industrial Magnetics / Flaig *FXE-TT* https://www.magnetics.com/products/fxe-tt , inspected 2026-10-29, **Features** headings *Two-Step Magnetizing Cycles*, *Current Control System*, *Lifting Eye Safety Switch*, *Signal Lights*, *Dual Control Interface* and *Autonomy*.

- The OEM describes **Pick Up** (initial force/test or destacking) versus **Full Mag** (maximum lifting power before the full lift); this is an example of separating a preliminary attachment check from full load transfer, **not** a universal numerical proof-load method.
- The OEM describes a **lifting-eye safety switch** that inhibits a commanded magnet OFF while the chain remains loaded; the operator must first have the load supported and lifting chain free of load. This is an **inspectable architecture pattern** that addresses accidental release at the actual lifting interface, rather than merely checking a normal controller's `MAG_ON` bit.
- A separate **magnetizing-current monitor** can detect a broken cable during switching. It is **not** a measurement of actual pull force, workpiece magnetic permeability, plate thickness or the mechanical attachment.
- The manufacturer says the electro-permanent system retains magnetic hold through power loss, while still requiring safe physical support before release. **No functional-safety performance level, diagnostic coverage, stop distance, installed machine configuration or tested release behavior** was established from this product page. The lifting-eye switch's exact channel topology and failure behavior are **UNKNOWN** without the complete selected manual.
- **Human factors:** An automatic release inhibit tied to load support makes the correct sequence easier than a procedural reminder; nevertheless, an operator must not have to enter a suspended-load/drop zone to clear an inhibit or reset a controller.

**Transfer distinction:** A *lifting-eye switch* is not a generic vacuum-switch replacement, and a robot's `at rest` bit is not a lifting-eye unloaded witness. On a press-brake gravity ram, analogous positive mechanical support is a separate machine-specific engineering function, **not** permission to transplant a magnet controller to the hydraulic circuit.
