# 4400-TRAPPED-KEY-01 — Eight paper-only technician decisions
**2026-10-30 | PUBLIC, UNSCORED | not a blind evaluator | no machine operation**

Use after [sequence/group LOTO original-source audit](../research/4400_TRAPPED_KEY_01_FORTRESS_ISO14119_SEQUENCE_PERSONNEL_KEY_VS_GROUP_LOTO_SOURCE_AUDIT_2026-10-30.md) and [PTU/PTL/override original-source audit](../research/4400_TRAPPED_KEY_02_FORTRESS_2025_PTU_PTL_ESCAPE_OVERRIDE_AND_COMMISSIONING_OEM_AUDIT_2026-10-30.md). For each case, write **hazard → claimed safe condition → independent authority → final physical element → evidence needed → human-factor fix → reset/restart**. Label DOC-CONFIRMED vs INFERENCE vs UNKNOWN. These are teaching prompts, **not graded/hidden answers**.

1. **Press brake: electric pump OFF, accumulator charged.** An exchange key opens a maintenance gate. Explain why the key cannot establish zero hydraulic pressure or ram holding; identify what a qualified machine-specific witness must prove.
2. **Two maintenance workers, one personnel key.** The first worker returns it while the second is behind a guard. Distinguish a shared safety key from individual servicing LOTO and describe a practical protected multi-person scheme.
3. **Robot cell: 20-second timer and heavier payload.** The key is released after the timer. Identify the missing physical stop/hold evidence, timing assumptions and safe approach to revising the design.
4. **Mill: PTL guard module installed to keep coolant inside.** A salesperson calls it a 'safety lock.' Use exact OEM part/option and power-loss/monitoring evidence to decide whether personnel guard locking has been established.
5. **Technician leaves auxiliary release key inserted.** The guard opens despite normal release inhibition. Describe management control, independent detection, physical stopping and a less inconvenient recovery workflow.
6. **Person inside cell uses escape release.** Describe the difference between initiating a stop signal and demonstrating safe final-element state; require external reset sightline and a fresh start.
7. **Duplicated code from another machine.** A replacement key opens the wrong access lock. Propose a site key-code register, spare-key custody, change control and inspection that is easier than improvisation.
8. **Three crews change shifts on a large machine.** A supervisor proposes a single personal lock plus sign-in sheet. Compare with OSHA's May 21, 2021 group-LOTO interpretation; preserve each worker's control and a clear handover procedure.

## Paper-only acceptance checklist
A useful answer must reject a key, HMI lamp, PLC bit or LinuxCNC/HAL command as standalone proof of physical stop/hold, account for **all** energy sources and stored energy, distinguish personnel key from OSHA personal lock where servicing LOTO applies, name the exact OEM option, preserve accessible escape, and require a deliberate rearm/restart. Do not calculate generic pressure, stopping time, PL or safety distance. If a minimum safe-to-operate threshold is unproven, **do not operate with people exposed**; any later experimental activity must be independently approved, physically isolated/remote and keep people outside the danger zone.
