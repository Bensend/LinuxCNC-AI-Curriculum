# 4400-WATERJET-01 — Jet OFF, pump OFF, pressure zero and safe access are different propositions

**2026-10-27 | 4400 cross-machine safety course | NON-GRADUATED | documentary-only**

## Bounded question and prior-work check
Can a CNC waterjet's software PAUSE, cutting-head interlock, pump stop or emergency-stop request be treated as proof that the high-pressure circuit is safe to service? **Pre-source prediction (INFERENCE): no**: ordinary command, pump drive, actual jet, stored pressure and physical access are distinct; bleed devices and independent verification are needed. ## Four-layer control / energy boundary
1. **Ordinary control:** LinuxCNC G-code/M-code, HMI PAUSE and normal pump/jet commands can request cut/flow changes. A GUI state or motion STOP is **not** a safety pressure witness.
2. **Safety-related control:** independently qualified access interlocks, protective stop and restart prevention, selected for the actual risk assessment. The ProtoMAX manual describes a lid switch but **does not in the inspected passage establish a PL/SIL or allow transfer of that switch's design** to another machine.
3. **Physical final elements:** pump motor drive, high-pressure shutoff/bleed, valves, lines, attenuators and nozzle. A stopped pump is not necessarily a proven zero-pressure line; an automatic bleed is an engineered mechanism that can fail or be isolated from a trapped segment.
4. **Personnel maintenance isolation:** qualified source lockout, safe pressure release by exact OEM method, independent verification of affected zones and prevention of inadvertent re-pressurization. This is separate from short-duration production pause.

## Failure paths and discriminating observations (INFERENCE)
| False assurance | Credible failure path | Required independent witness |
|---|---|---|
| HMI says JET OFF | Jet command deasserted, but pump/high-pressure line remains charged or nozzle valve leaks | Actual pressure/flow state in affected physical zone, independently qualified access state |
| Lid opens and motion/pump stop | Interlock changes control state but a trapped high-pressure segment remains | As-built hydraulic/water schematic, pressure bleed path and qualified zero-energy check |
| Product says 1-second bleed | Another model or failed bleed/blocked line has different pressure-decay behavior | Exact model/edition/valve topology and qualified observed pressure-decay evidence, **no universal time** |
| Software pressure fault clear | Sensor healthy but pressure path downstream isolated or pressure transducer invalid | Sensor placement, pressure validity, final-element and trapped-zone inspection |
| Technician sees a pinhole | Injection injury may be internally severe despite small wound | **Immediate medical evaluation** and explicit high-pressure waterjet exposure handoff; never wait for symptoms |

## Safer-by-default human factors
Make lid, enclosure and accessible inspection points convenient; label high-pressure zones and service isolation clearly. Use guarded, readily identifiable OEM bleed/isolation controls rather than requiring improvisation around fittings. Provide a visible, **non-safety-credit** status summary that distinguishes PUMP STOPPED / BLEED REQUESTED / PRESSURE UNVERIFIED / ACCESS NOT AUTHORIZED. Preserve a waterjet medical-alert card at the machine and in first-aid response materials. Guard reinstall and deliberate restart should be simpler than defeating a switch. Never troubleshoot suspected leaks with fingers or tighten UHP fittings under pressure.
