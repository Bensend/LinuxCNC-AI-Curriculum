# 4400 technician exercise — Two stop-time tests that answer different questions
**2026-10-24 | Open-book, paper-only | Not an SSC3000 setup instruction or live-machine test.**

Use the [Rockford KSL252 source trace](../research/4400_SSC3000_DOWNSTROKE_DISTANCE_VS_UPSTROKE_BRAKE_MONITOR_SOURCE_TRAP_2026-10-24.md) and [ISO 13855:2024 scope audit](../research/4400_ISO13855_2024_SEPARATION_DISTANCE_SCOPE_STOP_TIME_AND_GRAVITY_EXCEPTION_2026-10-24.md). Do not copy the OEM's example stop angles, display values, warning percentages or procedures to an unknown machine.

**Exercise A:** A machine builder shows ten short upstroke brake-monitor readings and says the light curtain may move closer. Specify the different downstroke physical/geometry proposition and the missing end-to-end evidence.

**Exercise B:** Nine results cluster tightly, while one run stops much later. A colleague quotes the SSC3000 p. 71 instruction about unusually high readings. Identify the **purpose of that paragraph**, the unresolved cause of the high result, and why the result cannot be dropped from a different protective-distance argument without independent evidence of invalid measurement.

**Exercise C:** A crankshaft encoder reports stopped, but a mechanical link breaks and the slide keeps moving. Identify the sensor target and the actual hazardous-motion witness.

**Exercise D:** A hydraulic press brake has independent Y1/Y2 axes. An integrator imports a part-revolution clutch/brake press's STM angle, fault setpoint and OSHA 1910.217 method. Identify the scope transfer errors and missing physical proof.

**Exercise E:** A guard is positioned conservatively but requires time-consuming removal for every routine tooling adjustment; staff bypass it. Propose a design/process improvement that keeps independent protection intact and makes correct use easier than defeat.

**Exercise F:** A light curtain and controller response are measured, but final-element valve dynamics, pressure decay and gravity holding are not. Trace which `PHY` claims remain unproven and which require mechanical or energy-isolation measures.

For each, write `DOC-CONFIRMED / INFERENCE / UNKNOWN / NOT RUN`, the missing instrument/diagram/standard and a falsifier. Do not self-score the sealed 2520–25F0 evaluations. Selected-machine `VAL/PT` remain NOT RUN; if physical safeguards cannot establish the minimum safe-to-operate threshold, do not operate with people exposed. Any future test must be isolated/remote and qualified with people outside the danger zone.
