# 4400-RCD-01 — Residual-current waveform, DC blinding and drive-specific protective-device selection
**2026-10-26 | original manufacturer/regulatory evidence | NON-GRADUATED | no selected-machine electrical approval**

## Concrete question and novelty check
If a builder retrofits a VFD or servo drive to a LinuxCNC machine and an existing RCD/GFCI trips, can they replace it with a higher-threshold device, disable the EMC filter, or assume a Type A “high-immunity” device is safe for every inverter? **No universal substitution is justified.** This is a distinct *residual-current waveform and receiver compatibility* investigation, not a rediscovery of PANEL-01 multi-feed/SCCR, BOND-01 door protective-earth continuity, or DCB-01 common-DC-bus stored energy. Those prior audits remain prerequisites.

## Causal failure paths — evidence labels
**DOC-CONFIRMED (OEM):** an inverter may inject PE current with AC, pulsating DC, smooth DC and higher-frequency components depending on product/topology. RCD sensing characteristics differ. Exact PowerFlex 527 manufacturer instructions prohibit upstream Type A/AC as a contact-protection RCD/RCM for that product; a different OEM's ATV12 FAQ can recommend Type A SI for its own context. **There is no actual contradiction** once device family, supply, waveform and use case are held fixed. Treating the recommendations as interchangeable is the dangerous transfer.

**INFERENCE (not machine-validated):** a technician who repeatedly encounters nuisance trips may bypass the RCD, remove a filter/PE bond, raise a trip threshold or share more drives downstream, possibly defeating required shock/fault protection. A Type A device designed for immunity to nuisance pulses is **not thereby a Type B smooth-DC sensor**. A breaker that stops tripping is not proof of fault coverage. Conversely, an RCD trip is an electrical protective action, not an independently proven controlled stop or gravity-load hold: unexpected power removal may worsen a vertical-load hazard.

## Four separately witnessed propositions
1. **Protective earthing:** actual PE conductors/bonding remain continuous/effective through panel, door, motor frame, moving cable and any filters. No generic resistance number or continuity-beep approval.
2. **Residual-current protection:** qualified electrician documents exact inverter make/model/edition, supply topology, installed RCD/RCM type, waveform compatibility, upstream/downstream coordination, manufacturer's instructions, applicable jurisdiction and approved acceptance evidence.
3. **Maintenance isolation:** identify and lock out all electrical/other sources; qualified verification of deenergized parts and stored energy. RCD, STO, E-stop and LinuxCNC/HAL “OFF” are not lockout.
4. **Physical machine response:** independent safety design validates what loss of electrical power does to hazardous motion, hydraulic pressure, suspended loads, guards and restart; do not borrow an RCD tripping curve as Y1/Y2 stop-time evidence.

## Practical human-factors design
Make the compliant choice easy: label each drive with its **approved RCD/RCM compatibility and PE requirements**, keep exact OEM instructions and qualified installation review beside the panel schematic, provide separately identified and lockable isolation points, use a documented troubleshooting route for nuisance trips, and avoid unmarked “temporary” filter/PE modifications. An operator should never need to defeat shock protection to resume production.
