# 4400 line-side live and dead-front access: ten paper technician challenges
**2026-10-24 | NO energized work or physical test.**
Read [original OSHA 2006 source audit](../research/4400_OSHA_2006_DEAD_FRONT_UPSTREAM_LIVE_DISCONNECT_PANEL_SERVICE_AUDIT_2026-10-24.md). For each case classify exposure by **task, geometry, guarding, source and qualification**, not the position of one switch.

| ID | Scenario | Expected boundary |
|---|---|---|
| DF-01 | Local disconnect locked open; load side tested zero; intact dead-front guards line side. | Qualified task-specific review; not automatically all-panel deenergized, but guarded work may be permissible under OSHA 2006 scenario. |
| DF-02 | Technician removes dead-front for access to line-side lugs. | Live parts newly exposed; upstream deenergization or justified qualified energized-work protections. |
| DF-03 | Employee asserts upstream isolation is always mandatory even if all energized parts fully guarded. | Reject absolute claim; OSHA requires case-specific exposure/feasibility assessment. |
| DF-04 | Worker says a live supply behind a loose cover is “dead front.” | Guard effectiveness unknown; HOLD. |
| DF-05 | Upstream disconnect 9 ft above floor, job lasts 30 minutes. | Height/duration do not waive needed lockout. |
| DF-06 | Drill penetrates enclosure near protected live feed. | Task introduces conductive-object exposure; revise assessment, do not rely on static guard assumptions. |
| DF-07 | Door swings toward worker near live terminals. | Secure hinged panels and preserve safe workspace. |
| DF-08 | Operator servicing machine is not touching panel; only local disconnect is OFF. | OSHA 2006 letter explicitly lacked enough machine hazard facts; require full energy map. |
| DF-09 | Normal CNC controller disables motion and reports safe. | Not lockable electrical isolation; gravity/hydraulic risks independent. |
| DF-10 | Planner cites 1910.147 alone to waive Subpart S electrical testing. | Reject; 1910.147 electrical-utilization exposure exclusion and 1910.333 qualified verification must be reconciled. |

**Human-factors check:** Record whether routine maintenance can be performed with dead-front intact and without defeating an interlock. Improve access/segregation if not. Do not perform a live cabinet exercise or claim a machine acceptance result. G3/G4 NOT AUTHORIZED.
